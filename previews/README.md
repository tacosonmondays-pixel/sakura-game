Dev-only preview pages (not part of the game build). Each area may add `previews/<area>.html` + `previews/<area>.js`
to look at its work in isolation, then screenshot it with `node e2e/shot.mjs /previews/<area>.html out.png`.

- `attack-shapes.html` — the attack shapes owner board (docs/specs/ATTACK_SHAPES.md §7.1): ten animated mock-ups on a sample road, stepped at 60 Hz with the sim's direction table (`src/sim/dirs.js`); `?speed=0.5`, `?shot=1` (one column), `?reel=1&manual=1` (`window.__advance(n)`, `window.__pose()`). `node e2e/attack-shapes-board.mjs <dir>` writes the board images and the video (`docs/review/attack-shapes/`).
- `attacks.html` — attack FX gallery (every girl side by side) and focus view (one girl, any build); see docs/ATTACK_FX.md.
- `campus.html` — the Gate Arc campus look at night (`THEMES.campus`, src/render/city/campusProps.js) on two fixture boards from `campusMaps.js` (never in MAPS or STAGES): `?map=court|track&blackout=1&cycle=1&quality=…&cam=shelter&grey=1&clean=1&frame=battle|full&bands=1`; the "Lights out" button (or `window.__lights(on)`) sends the `ruleState` event that drives blackout dimming; `window.__terrainStats(q)` for the phone budget.
- `city.html` — the Gate Arc city look (`THEMES.city`, src/render/city/) on two fixture boards from `cityMaps.js` (never in MAPS or STAGES): `?map=downtown|canal&quality=…&cam=rift|shelter&grey=1&clean=1&frame=battle|full&bands=1` (framed like the battle by default: between its HUD and tower bar, `bands=1` draws them), plus `window.__terrainStats(q)` for the phone budget.
- `highway.html` — the Gate Arc Step 4 looks (S4a) on four fixture boards from `highwayMaps.js` (never in MAPS or STAGES): the mountain highway (`district: 'highway'`, src/render/city/highwayProps.js) at its toll plaza in frost, Azami's checkpoint at dusk with the barrier arm on the lane schedule (`city.barriers`), the tunnel mouth under snow, and a frozen village of the far north on `snow` (src/render/northProps.js, the Brigade's dressing in src/render/city/brigadeProps.js): `?map=tolls|checkpoint|tunnel|north&wave=1|2&brigade=1&quality=…&grey=1&clean=1&frame=battle|full&bands=1`; `window.__wave()` steps the wave (the barrier follows), `window.__terrainStats(q)` for the phone budget.
- `drowned.html` — the Gate Arc Step 5 looks (S5a) on four fixture boards from `drownedMaps.js` (never in MAPS or STAGES): the Drowned Line (`theme: 'drowned'`, src/render/city/drownedProps.js) on its flooded platform with the subway spawn at a bore's OUT mouth, the Line 2 tunnels in a blackout (the red emergency lamps pulse, the rest dims), the pump station with the Noon Gate's turning shards and two steam valves, and the reservoir park (`district: 'park'`, src/render/city/parkProps.js) round the great willow by night (or `willowDay`: by day in fog), with stand-in Noon machines: `?map=platform|tunnels|pumps|willow|willowDay&t=15&active=1&machines=1&quality=…&grey=1&clean=1&frame=battle|full&bands=1`; `window.__active(on)` sends the `ruleState` event (the blackout; fast-forward into the ACTIVE phase with `t`: tunnels 15, pumps 11), `window.__machines()` sends the machines (two break apart), `window.__terrainStats(q)` for the phone budget.
- `viaductMaps.js` (no page) — the Gate Arc Step 6 viaduct fixture (S6v, `RAISE` / `LOWER` in src/core/track.js): a straight viaduct with the return road under its flat top, never in MAPS or STAGES; the sim, lockstep, world and render tests run it until 6-7 raises a real one.
- `vesper.html` — the Gate Arc Step 6 looks (S6a) on seven fixture boards from `vesperMaps.js` (never in MAPS or STAGES): Vesperline frozen (`theme: 'vesper'`, `scenery.ruined: 'frozen'`, src/render/city/vesperProps.js) on the causeway (light-bridges, prism batteries), the sky-rail (a hand-written viaduct span with piers along it, the broken span's pod, an under-crossing), the museum in a blackout, the tower ring (transit pads, the cleaning robot), the Spindle's foot (two Vesper Gates, the shelter's warm window), the Starfall Deck at night, and the living city on a morning board: `?map=causeway|skyrail|museum|towers|spindle|starfall|living&t=15&active=1&machines=1&quality=…&grey=1&clean=1&frame=battle|full&bands=1`; `window.__active(on)` (the museum's blackout: fast-forward with `t=15`), `window.__machines()` sends the Step 5 machines and the Step 6 stand-ins (the Rime Wisp, the Gatekeeper), `window.__terrainStats(q)` for the phone budget.
