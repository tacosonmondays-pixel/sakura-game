# Sakura Sentinels: asset wishlist (3D models and illustrations)

This list is for the owner, who makes 3D models in Tripo (text or image → 3D) and illustrations in NovelAI. It covers
every 3D model the game uses or needs and every illustration it is missing, with a ready-to-paste prompt for each,
what to export, and how to send the files in.

Written Oct 31, 2026 from `release/v0.1.66` (v0.1.66 "Mirrors, Curses and the Gear Update"). The sources of truth are
`src/data/units*.js` (the girls), `src/data/enemies.js` (the bestiary), `src/data/maps.js` / `stages.js` (maps and decor),
`src/data/gearSets.js` (gear), `public/models/**/manifest.json` (what has a GLB today), `src/art/portraits.js` and
`src/data/story/backgrounds.js` (drawn art), `docs/MODELS.md` (the model pipeline).

**Prompt rules (HANDOFF §6, never bend).** Every prompt is written only from the game's own character descriptions.
Students are never sexualised: modest school outfits, knee-length skirts, no swimsuits. Adult staff wear
professional outfits and are never framed around their bodies. No gore anywhere (enemies "poof", topple or
go home). No other game's, show's or artist's name in any prompt. Generators only ever receive the game's own art
(for image → 3D, use the girl's own `public/art/portraits/<id>-full.webp`).

## Priorities and status words

| word | meaning |
|---|---|
| **P1** | missing and visible: the game shows a stand-in, a flat drawing, another model's mesh in new colours, or a signboard |
| **P2** | a placeholder works today (a temporary Blender cage figure or a shared family mesh) and a real model is wanted |
| **P3** | nice to have: there is a good model or drawing, or the thing is small on screen |
| has GLB | a finished game file exists (Meshy import or a recent Blender build) |
| placeholder | a temporary file exists (Blender cage chibi, family GLB variant, procedural mesh) |
| MISSING | nothing: a procedural stand-in, a flat 2D drawing or another model's mesh |

The owner's standing decision (HANDOFF §5): Meshy/generator figures are the main look; the Blender chibis are
accepted as temporary. So every Blender girl is P2, and the girls already imported from Meshy are P3.

# Part 1: 3D models (Tripo)

## 1.0 The style block and the export sheet (read once)

**Style block** (already inside every prompt below; if Tripo shortens a long prompt, keep the part before the style
words, since the style is repeated everywhere):

> soft anime chibi style, sakura palette, clean rounded low-poly shapes, flat cel-shaded colours, game-ready, single object, no base, no text

For the girls, the approved look is `docs/art/approved/chibi-style-hikari-turnaround.webp` (the game's own figure):
about 2.5–3 heads tall, a very large round head, small soft body, short limbs, small hands and feet, huge shiny eyes
with thick dark upper lashes, light blush, blunt bangs with pointed strands, modest outfits copied from her own
illustration. **Best results: image → 3D** with her own `public/art/portraits/<id>-full.webp` (or the approved
turnaround for the proportions) plus her text prompt. Do not feed anything that is not the game's own art.

**Negative prompt / "avoid"** (paste where Tripo offers one): `realistic proportions, long legs, revealing clothes,
swimsuit, short skirt, cleavage, gore, blood, weapons pointed at camera, text, logo, base, pedestal, multiple
characters, extra limbs`.

### Export sheet

| kind | export | triangles (we decimate down to these) | textures | pose | clips we need (exact names) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| girl / hero chibi | GLB (binary glTF) | full **≤ 20k**, we make the LOD **≈ 9k** | base colour 2048² from Tripo (we ship 1024² WebP); normal and metal/roughness optional | **A-pose** (arms 45° down) or T-pose, feet apart, facing front, in-place | `idle` (loop ≈1.5 s), `walk` (loop ≈1 s, in place), `attack_<kind>` (one-shot ≈0.6 s; the kind is in her row), `cheer` (≈1.2 s), `hurt` (≈0.4 s); `victory` and `pickup` optional (they fall back to `cheer`) |
| companion (Kon, Kaze, doll, Big Yellow) | GLB | 2–4k (Big Yellow 6k) | 512² (Big Yellow 1024²) | neutral | `appear`, `idle`, `move`, `attack` |
| enemy, normal | GLB | ≈ 5k | 512² | A-pose / neutral | `move` (loop), `idle` (loop), `hit` (≈0.3 s), `death` (≈0.8 s, a poof, topple or wilt, never gore), `special` (cast / roar / blink wind-up); flying dragons also `walk` |
| enemy, elite | GLB | ≈ 8k | 1024² | same | same |
| boss / miniboss | GLB | ≈ 12k | 1024² | same | same |
| titan | GLB | ≈ 20k | 1024² + normal | same | same |
| placed prop / trap / turret | GLB, one node per moving part, named as in its row | 300–2,500 | 512² or vertex colours | rest pose | usually none (the game animates the parts) |
| map set piece | GLB | 500–4k (landmarks up to 6k) | 512²–1024², one atlas per chapter if you can | rest | none (trees sway in the shader) |
| gear item (icon source) | GLB | ≤ 3k | 512² | front three-quarter | none (we bake a flat 3D-style icon in Blender) |

**Rig.** Tripo's automatic humanoid rig is fine. Keep one skeleton per figure with a floor root; we rename the bones to
ours (`root, hips, spine, chest, neck, head, upper_arm_L/R, lower_arm_L/R, hand_L/R, upper_leg_L/R, lower_leg_L/R,
foot_L/R`, plus hair / coat chains) and retarget our own clip library, so Tripo's animations are a bonus, not a must.
If a Tripo animation is used, bake it **in place** (no root motion) and name the clip exactly as in the sheet.
Hand props (sword, bow, coilgun) may be part of the mesh; a prop that must swap or glow (Tenka's coilgun) is better
as its own GLB with a `hand_R` socket.

**Scale and orientation.** Any unit is fine as long as one batch is consistent: we normalise every figure to its
battle height (students 0.9, cage heroes 0.98, imported figures 1.224 game units, stored in `asset.extras.height`).
"Size vs chibi" in the enemy tables is the enemy's height divided by a student chibi's. Pivot at the floor between the
feet, Y up, the figure facing the front view. No base or pedestal, no ground plane, no lights or cameras in the file.

**Faces.** Painted eyes in the texture are fine (the game draws blinks and the ^ ^ / > < states over measured eye boxes).
Keep the eyes clear of hair: the battle camera looks down at 48°, so bangs must not cover the eyes from slightly above.

# Part 2: Illustrations (NovelAI)

**How the prompts are written.** Quality tags first, then who (role, never another work's character), then the look
from her own bio and palette, then framing. No artist tags and no other works' names. Students: school-appropriate,
fully clothed, knee-length skirts, no swimsuits. Adults: professional outfits, framed at the face and outfit, never
the body. Paste the **Undesired Content** line given with each table. If NovelAI drifts on colours, add her hex colours
in words (e.g. "sakura-pink hair, teal eyes"); the hair and eye colours below come from her palette in `src/data/units.js`.

**Sizes.** Portrait master 3:4, at least **1536×2048** (we cut `-full` 768×1024, `-card` 300×500, `-bust` 512×512,
`-thumb` 160×160 from it). The stand (`-cut`, 1000 px tall, and `-cut-s`, 360 px) needs a **separate full-body
standing pose, head to shoes, on a plain flat white or light-grey background** so we can cut it out cleanly (same
outfit, same prompt + `full body, standing, simple background, white background`). Story backgrounds and lobby
scenes 16:9, at least **1920×1080** (2560×1440 better). Banners 16:9 with the left third calm for text.

# Part 3: How to send them to us

1. **Names.** Lower case, the ids from these tables.
   - Girls: `<id>.glb` (`kogane.glb`, `azami.glb`; the duo `kohaku.glb`, `tsubaki.glb`). Several tries: `kogane_v2.glb`.
   - Enemies: `<enemy id>.glb` (`brigade_trooper.glb`, `fp_eclipse_wyrm.glb`).
   - Props, companions, map pieces, gear: the id in the row (`suzu_bell.glb`, `big_yellow.glb`, `oldLamp.glb`,
     `gear_spirit_hair.glb`, i.e. `gear_<set>_<slot>`).
   - Portraits: one master per girl, `<id>-master.png` (3:4) and `<id>-stand.png` (full body on a plain background). We
     cut the six game files.
   - Story places: `<id>.png` with the id from §2.3.
   Also send a short `notes.txt` per batch: the prompt you used, the Tripo settings (face limit, texture size, style
   option) or the NovelAI seed, and anything you want changed.
2. **Where.** Upload the files to the session (or a zip). We keep sources outside the shipped folder:
   `tools/art/src/tripo/<batch>/` for 3D and `tools/art/src/novelai/<batch>/` for art. Only the processed files go to
   `public/`. Never include third-party images or other games' art in a batch.
3. **Sizes.** 3D: a GLB up to 60 MB is fine to send (Tripo's raw output); we decimate. Textures as they come (2048² is
   ideal). Illustrations: PNG, the sizes in Part 2; no upscaling artefacts, no watermark.
4. **What we do on import.**
   - **Inspect:** `npx @gltf-transform/cli inspect file.glb` (triangles, textures, skin, clips).
   - **Rig check:** one skin, a floor root, the humanoid bones present; we rename them to the game's names
     (`tools/meshy/lib/rig_names.py` style) and retarget the clip library (`tools/meshy/lib/retarget.py`), so the clips
     come out as `idle`, `walk`, `attack_<kind>`, `cheer`, `hurt` (+ `victory`, `pickup`). Enemies get
     `move idle hit death special`, companions `appear idle move attack`.
   - **Decimate and pack:** to the budgets in the export sheet with Blender (`tools/meshy/lib/lowpoly.py`, the face
     protected) or `npx @gltf-transform/cli simplify in.glb out.glb --ratio 0.25 --error 0.001`, then textures to
     WebP 1024² (`gltf-transform resize` + `webp`) and **KHR_mesh_quantization** (`gltf-transform quantize`, or
     `gltfpack -i in.glb -o out.glb -si 0.25 -kn -km`; **no `-c`**: the game has no meshopt or Draco decoder, so
     compressed files would not load). Our own `lib/pack.py` does the same packing for the phone build.
   - **Eyes and outline:** we measure the painted eye boxes (`lib/eyes.py`) for blinking and set the outline tint.
   - **Register:** a manifest entry (`public/models/characters/manifest.json` or the enemy/companion/prop manifest),
     the full + `.lod.glb` pair, `source: 'tripo'`, and the stand-in id removed from `STANDIN_UNITS` for Kogane / Norika.
   - **Tests and look:** `npx vitest run tests/models` (clips, bones, sizes, every enemy resolves) and contact sheets
     (`previews/models-v2.html`, `previews/enemies-v2.html`) for the owner board before anything ships.
   - Every new model or picture is display only (no sim change, no `COOP_PROTOCOL` bump) and ships with a patch note.
5. **Who answers what.** One batch at a time; we report back with a contact sheet (battle size and close-up) and
   anything that needs a re-roll (eyes hidden by bangs from the 48° camera, a skirt too short, hands fused to props).
