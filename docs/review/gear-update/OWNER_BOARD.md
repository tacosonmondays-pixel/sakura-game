# Gear update: owner board

Oct 30, 2026. The full spec is `docs/specs/GEAR_UPDATE.md`. Each question has a recommended default; **"default all"**
takes every one. The build follows the defaults until you say otherwise.

---

**Q0. A set is five pieces. DECIDED (you, Oct 30).**
Every set covers all five slots: Charm, Ribbon, Shoes, the hair piece and the necklace. This replaces the Oct 21 plan
of two-piece sets on the new pieces.

---

**Q1. Set bonuses at 2 / 4 / 5 pieces?**
**Default: yes.** 2 pieces give the set's stat, 4 pieces its passive, 5 pieces a stronger passive and the set's look (a
crest on her card, her battle ring in the set colour). One passive per girl at most; 2 + 2 + 1 gives two stats.
*Other option:* 3 / 5 (3 pieces the passive, 5 stronger): simpler, fewer ways to mix.

**Q2. These six sets to start, one per role, each from its own place?**

| Set | Role | Drops from | Its 4-piece passive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spirit Squad | support | Titan Trials: Hyakki Daimyo | Big Cheer: every 20 s, girls in her aura deal +12 % damage for 5 s |
| Freeze Tag | control | Titan Trials: Gloom Regent | You're It: enemies she slows, freezes, stuns or holds take +6 % damage from every girl |
| Geometry Set | precision | Titan Trials: Eclipse Wyrm | Right Angle: every 4th attack is a sure crit |
| Party Popper | crowd | Titan Trials: Foundry Titan | Confetti Burst: every 6th attack bursts on the enemies around |
| Treasurer | money | Market Canal (Bounty) | Bake Sale: +10 % coins from enemies she defeats |
| Power Bank | abilities | Total Assault | Supercharge: her ⚡ ability hits 15 % harder and charges 10 % faster |

**Default: yes.** Each set has its own school look with a tech touch (pom-poms and light-up trainers, a chalk-line tag
game, a geometry kit, party poppers, a calculator, a power bank). A full Legendary set takes about 2 weeks of focused
farming at Mayhem (about 4 at Nightmare).

**Q3. Clubs stay on top of sets?**
**Default: yes**, as you said on Oct 21: clubs go on any of the five pieces and count across all five (3 + 2 gives one
trick and two club stats). Sets and clubs stack.

**Q4. The new pieces are the "Hair Clip" and the "Necklace", and they only come in sets?**
**Default: yes.** Ribbons already include bows, scrunchies and hairpins, so the hair piece is a clip. Today's drops keep
rolling Charm, Ribbon and Shoes. The new pieces' main stats include the support stats (Aura Power and Aura Radius on
the Hair Clip, Buff Duration and Control Strength on the Necklace) next to damage stats.

**Q5. Five pieces make a fully geared girl about twice as strong. OK?**
**Default: yes.** +60 % gear stats is about ×2 damage, because attack, speed and crit multiply. Balance round 5 is
re-checked on top so everyone stays close to Kage; nobody is nerfed.
*Other option:* the two new pieces at 60 % strength (about ×1.5).

**Q6. Keep the very top of Freeplay (the Eclipse Wyrm, wave 250) out of reach, even with full LR gear?**
**Default: yes.** If our test runs show LR gear beating it, the LR numbers come down a little before launch, never
anything players already own.
*Other option:* let full LR teams beat it.

**Q7. "Multiple girl loadouts" means saved teams and gear loadouts?**
**Default: both.** Up to 5 saved teams on Formation and Stage Prep, and up to 3 gear loadouts (A / B / C) per girl. A
team remembers which loadout each girl uses, so Kage can wear her boss set in one team and her crowd set in another;
a switch that takes a piece from another girl says so first and can be undone. Teams ship first, on their own.
*Other options:* teams only (gear stays as it is), or gear loadouts only.

**Q8. LR gear: clearly stronger than UR ("LR's should be more op!")?**
**Default: yes, with these numbers.**
- Made by **Limit Break**: a rainbow UR piece at +12 + 1 **Nightglass Lens** + 150,000 coins. The Lens comes from a
  weekly Titan Trials task, Freeplay wave 225, achievements and events (about one a week). LR never drops and is never
  in a box.
- Every number ×1.25 over UR, max **+18** (two more substat upgrades), a third Needle stitch, a second Collar Pin.
- Each LR piece raises her caps: aura the most (Aura Power up to 45 %), crit the least (Crit Rate up to 80 %).
- Five LR pieces of one set unlock its **LR form** (for example Right Angle · Nightglass: every 3rd attack a sure crit).
- About ×1.2 damage per LR piece; a full LR set about ×2.2 a full UR set.
*Other option:* milder (numbers ×1.12 over UR): a full LR set about ×1.9.

**Q9. Set Boxes (sets that come pre-loaded)?**
**Default:** a box holds all five pieces of one set at Mythic or Legendary, +0. You pick each piece's main stat; the
substats are random. Choose-your-set boxes come from events and milestones; a titan set's box sits on the Titan shelf
(the Titan of the Week's set, Mythic, 1 a week) and comes once with each titan's Nightmare first clear (Legendary).
Never for gems, never UR or LR, so farming each set's own source still matters.

**Q10. Pins: two kinds?**
**Default: yes.** The **Tie Pin** turns a piece's main stat into its best possible roll; the **Collar Pin** does the same
for one substat, and rerolls keep it. Gold, rainbow and LR pieces only; one of each per piece (LR pieces take two
Collar Pins). From the Bounty Exchange, the Titan shelf, Titan Trials chests, Freeplay, achievements and events; never
for gems.
*Other option:* one pin that does both on a piece.

**Q11. Freeze Tag's bonus also works a little on bosses (+3 %, half the normal amount)?**
**Default: yes.** It is the one set bonus that helps the whole team against bosses, so it stays small and is measured.

**Q12. Raise the Aura Power cap from 15 % to 25 %?**
**Default: yes.** It is now a main stat, and one gold main already reaches 16 %. Nothing anyone has gets weaker.

**Q13. Names OK?**
**Default: keep.** Hair Clip, Necklace; Spirit Squad, Freeze Tag, Geometry Set, Party Popper, Treasurer, Power Bank and
their passives; Limit Break and the Nightglass Lens for LR gear; Set Box; Tie Pin and Collar Pin; Team 1–5; Loadout
A / B / C.

**Q14. Release order?**
**Default:** (1) a quiet save update first; (2) teams and loadouts, soon; (3) the two new pieces, the six sets, Set
Boxes and pins, after balance round 5 (a co-op update); (4) LR gear, with (3) or right after it (a co-op update). Each
ships as its own update when it is ready.
