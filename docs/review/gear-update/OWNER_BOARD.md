## Answered

**Q0. A set is five pieces. DECIDED (you, Oct 30).**
Every set covers all five slots: Charm, Ribbon, Shoes, the hair piece and the necklace.

**Q1. Set bonuses at 2 / 4 / 5 pieces? Answer: default (yes).**
2 pieces give the set's stat, 4 its passive, 5 a stronger passive and the set's look. One passive per girl.
*Added with your Oct 30 design:* the 5-piece bonus also **removes the cap on the set's signature stat** (Geometry Set
crit, Power Bank ability haste, Spirit Squad aura power, Freeze Tag control strength, Treasurer money, Party Popper
splash).

**Q2. The six sets, one per role, each from its own place? Answer: default (yes).**

| Set | Role | Drops from | Its 4-piece passive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spirit Squad | support | Titan Trials: Hyakki Daimyo | Big Cheer: every 20 s, girls in her aura deal +12 % damage for 5 s |
| Freeze Tag | control | Titan Trials: Gloom Regent | You're It: enemies she slows, freezes, stuns or holds take +6 % damage from every girl |
| Geometry Set | precision | Titan Trials: Eclipse Wyrm | Right Angle: every 4th attack is a sure crit |
| Party Popper | crowd | Titan Trials: Foundry Titan | Confetti Burst: every 6th attack bursts on the enemies around |
| Treasurer | money | Market Canal (Bounty) | Bake Sale: +10 % coins from enemies she defeats |
| Power Bank | abilities | Total Assault | Supercharge: her ⚡ ability hits 15 % harder and charges 10 % faster |

**Q3. Clubs stay on top of sets? Answer: default (yes).**

**Q4. The new pieces are the "Hair Clip" and the "Necklace", only from sets? Answer: default (yes).**

**Q5. Five pieces make a fully geared girl about twice as strong? Answer: default (yes).**
*Note added after your answer (review r1):* five pieces help crit girls the most. Girls whose damage cannot crit (Suzu's
traps) and burn or poison girls (Akane, Midori) would fall about 30 % behind Kage, so the update gives them their
average crit on that damage and a bigger share of gear ATK for burns, and any girl still behind is **raised** to stay
close to Kage. Nobody is lowered.

**Q6. The Eclipse Wyrm (Freeplay wave 250) with full LR gear? Answer: CHANGED (you, Oct 30).**
A team in full **blank LR** **can** beat it: "not too easy but not impossible", a hard, earned kill with the best
builds. We measure it with the balance round 5 tools on **24 test runs** (two maps × 12 seeds): the best full blank-LR
team should win a quarter to three quarters of them (your "1–3 of 4"), and we tune it to the low end (6–10 of 24) so the
LR accessories still have room to help. Full UR and set-LR teams should win none. Every run also records how close it
was, so the result does not hang on a lucky seed.

**Q7. "Multiple girl loadouts" = 5 saved teams and 3 gear loadouts per girl? Answer: default (both).**

**Q8. LR gear clearly stronger than UR? Answer: default (yes).**
Limit Break a rainbow UR +12 piece with a Nightglass Lens: numbers ×1.25 over UR, +18, a third Needle stitch, a second
Collar Pin, cap raises per LR piece (aura most, crit least), and the set's LR form with five LR pieces of one set.
*Added with your Oct 30 design:* at Limit Break you choose **Set LR** (keeps its set, as above) or **Blank LR** (no
set: much bigger numbers, ×1.56 over UR; five blank LR pieces **double every stat cap**, crit 75 % → 150 %; crit chance
above 100 % becomes **super crits**, a second, bigger crit). Today's pieces, which have no set, go straight to Blank LR.

**Q9. Set Boxes? Answer: default (yes).** All five pieces of one set at Mythic or Legendary, +0, mains picked, substats
random. Never for gems, never UR or LR. (The choose-a-set boxes offer all six sets.)

**Q10. Pins: the Tie Pin (main stat) and the Collar Pin (one substat)? Answer: default (yes).**

**Q11. Freeze Tag's bonus also works a little on bosses (+3 %)? Answer: default (yes).**

**Q12. Raise the Aura Power cap from 15 % to 25 %? Answer: default (yes).**

**Q13. Names OK? Answer: default (keep).**

**Q14. Release order? Answer: default.** A quiet save update first; then teams and loadouts; then the two new pieces,
the six sets, Set Boxes and pins after balance round 5 (a co-op update); then LR gear.
*Note added after your answer (review r1):* the sets update needs **one SQL paste from you** (one file, no keys) so
profile cards and Support Students can carry five pieces, set ids and the LR marker. Until then they show the three
classic pieces, as today.
*Note added (review r2):* balance round 5 and its LR rows come first (Q19); the gear update is tuned on top of them.

**Q19. LR units at least about 2.5–3× the best Kage? DECIDED (you, Oct 30): "LR floors, no nerfs".**
Every LR unit (today Kohaku & Tsubaki and Tenka) is at least about 2.5–3× the best tier-5 Kage, **on both a boss and a
crowd**. It is a floor, never a cap: an LR unit above 3× stays there, and nobody is lowered to get there. Balance round 5
is lifting the LR units now (its new LR rows); the gear update is tuned **on top of that, never before**, and checks the
floor again with five pieces and with LR gear.

## Open

**Q15. Today's gear stays "No set" for good?**
**Default: yes**, and it becomes the base of **blank LR**: a No-set rainbow piece at +12 Limit Breaks straight into
Blank LR (much bigger numbers, and five of them double every cap), giving nothing up. So the gear you already built
stays your path to the very top.
*Other options:* a one-time way to tag today's gold and rainbow pieces into a titan set; or a "set Armband" item that
moves any piece into a set.

**Q16. Veteran players get the new boxes once?**
**Default: yes.** When the sets update first loads: one **Legendary Set Box** of that titan's set for each titan you
already cleared on Nightmare (new players get it from that first clear), and the **welcome Mythic Set Box** for every
save past 3-5. Each box is paid once per save, whichever way it comes first (one mark per box), so nobody gets two.

**Q17. The Accessory Banner for LR personal accessories?**
**Default:** 120 gems a pull (as recruiting); the featured LR's personal accessory at 1 %; **a guaranteed one by the
80th pull** (the count carries over to that LR's next accessory banner); copies raise it R1 → R5; extra copies become
tokens for standard accessories; no spark; rates and the pity count always shown. Proposed first two: the
**Walkie-Talkie Pair** for Kohaku & Tsubaki (Target Call: her shells land instantly on the enemy Kohaku calls) and the
**Lab Goggles** for Tenka (Charged Rail: once a second her bolt lays a rail that hurts the enemies it touches).

*What it buys:* R1 makes her about 15–20 % stronger and unlocks the new mechanic; R5 about 40–50 %. With the LR floor
(Q19), an LR unit with R5 is at least about 3.5× the best Kage. The Wyrm stays a hard kill even then (never every run).

*What it costs* (each copy starts a new count):

| | Pulls on average | At most | Gems on average / at most | A free player's gems (1,700 a week) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| R1 | about 55 | 80 | about 6,600 / 9,600 | about 4 weeks (at most 6) |
| R5 | about 276 | 400 | about 33,000 / **48,000** | about 20 weeks (at most 28), pulling nothing else |

About 55 % of players get the accessory before the guarantee. For comparison, the LR girl herself is guaranteed by her
300th pull. **The accessory update waits for your answer here**: it would be the game's first new gem price.
*Other options:* pity at 60 (gentler: R1 about 45 pulls on average, R5 at most 300 pulls = 36,000 gems) or 100; or one
free R1 copy from each LR's bond story.

**Q18. The Build Coach plans only with what players earn by playing?**

button), her best set, stats, pins and clubs, and a build score with next steps. It never points to the Gem Shop or a
banner; an LR girl's personal accessory shows as information only.
*Other option:* also list the Gem Shop and banners as sources.
