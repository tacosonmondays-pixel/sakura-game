# Banners, LR, heroes, friends and the Workbench: the short version

Design only; nothing in the game has changed yet. The full spec is `SPEC.md` in this folder. Balance comes last, but every number
here is sized so free players stay fine.

## The banners

| Banner | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Student Spotlight** (gems) | Towers only. One featured SSR at 1.0% plus 3 rate-up SRs at 3% each. Changes every day at 7 PM Central. |
| **Guardian Spotlight** (gems) | The hero banner. One featured SSR hero plus 3 rate-up SRs (1 SR hero and 2 SR towers). Changes daily. |
| **LR Banner** (gems) | Its own tab, open every day. The LR unit is 0.5% (0.7% in her launch week), and that comes **out of** the normal 1.5% SSR rate, so it is the rarest pull and never a better pull than a Spotlight. No spark, but these pulls still earn spark points for other girls. **Legend Promise:** your 300th pull on her banner is her. |
| **Standard** (gems) | Standard girls only, never premium or LR. You pick its featured SSR (1.0%) from the 5 standard SSRs, and it has 3 SR rate-ups. Your first 10× after the update guarantees an SSR. |
| **Coin** (25,000 coins a pull) | Opens after 6-5. Mostly R and SR copies for Potential, with a tiny SSR chance. 20 pulls a week. |
| **Friendship** (100 Friend Points a pull) | Same idea, paid with the points friends earn you. |

Every banner follows your "1 select SSR + 3 SR rate-ups" rule, Coin and Friendship included. Pity stays at 90 and is shared by all
the gem banners. Coin and Friendship pulls never move pity or spark points, not even through duplicates, so coins and friends can
never stand in for gems.

## Standard or premium

| Standard (Standard banner + any day spark) | Premium (only on her Spotlight, spark on her day) | LR (only on her LR Banner, no spark) |
|---|---|---|
| Hikari, Luna, Hotaru, Kaede, Chika, and **every R and SR**, Norika included | Kanna, Yoriko, Seiran, Iroha, Ena, Sorano, Kogane | Tenka, Kohaku & Tsubaki |

Kanna, Yoriko, Seiran, Iroha and Ena arrive in v0.1.39, before banners exist. So today's banner is renamed "Open Enrollment"
(it never says "Standard" while they are in it), and they can still be sparked on any day until 30 days after banners arrive.

## UR and LR boosts

- **UR (★5) now gives a real boost:** +10% damage and +4% attack speed (about ×1.14). Support girls get +10% on their own
  thing instead (aura, coins, drones). No duplicates needed.
- **LR adds more on top:** +15% damage and +5% attack speed (about ×1.21), plus her LR ability form.
- **How you reach LR:** level 60 with all 5 gates, then ★5 (UR), then a one-time Limit Break fee, then a special solo stage you
  must beat with your own copy. First clear gives LR, 300 gems and a Recruit Ticket.
- In one line: you pull an LR unit, she starts in SSR form, and she climbs to UR and then LR.

## New heroes

- **Kogane** (SSR hero, premium): the academy's facilities crew chief in her walker, Big Yellow. Her ult plows enemies back up
  the road.
- **Norika** (SR hero, standard): the academy's traffic safety officer. She puts up barricades on the road, her ult drops more
  where you aim, and her whistle stops the whole line.
- Both are adults (heroes are always adults) and each has a full 25-level kit. **Marumi, Chigusa and Sorano stay towers**, as you
  asked on Oct 14 (the rolling bomb, the nature caster, and the firebird that hunts the whole map).
- Heroes still level for free in battle. A hero pull unlocks her, and duplicates give Potential. Banners never sell battle levels.

## Friend Support

Each Sensei lends one **Support Student**. You can borrow one friend's student in solo campaign, Bounty and Freeplay (not Titan
Trials for now). Both of you get 10 Friend Points once the run reaches wave 3. The caps are 50 a day for borrowing and 100 a day
for lending, so a typical player gets 3–5 Friendship pulls a week. A borrowed student is capped near your own level, and a run
with a friend can clear a stage for the story but cannot earn Hard or Nightmare medals, sweeps or achievements, so nobody can
skip the game on a friend's account.

## Tenka's banner

**"Tenka · Physics Club Premiere"**: her own banner for 7 days at 0.7%, with Miko, Umeko and Raika as SR rate-ups. After that she
stays in the daily LR tab at 0.5%. She ships with a small banner piece of her own, so she never waits for the full rotation. The
update before hers tells players to save gems. She sells for 70%, and her LR form comes later with her trial, "Thesis Defense".

## The Workbench ("keep it timed")

A new timed crafting bench next to the instant Craft Room. You start a craft, leave, and come back to collect: dice, books, Lock
Pins, gear boxes, Prism Dice. It has 2 slots (a 3rd after 6-5), 1–12 hour timers, no gem speed-ups, and weekly caps no higher
than the shop's.

## A free player's month (76.7 gem pulls, before patch-note gifts)

| Goal | Odds |
|---|---|
| Any SSR | about 1.6–1.8 a month (unchanged from today, plus a little from Coin and Friendship) |
| A featured girl you want | **54%** in 1 month, **91%** in 2, **certain by 200 pulls** with the spark |
| An LR unit | **32%** in 1 month, **64%** in 2, **80%** in 3, **certain by 300 pulls** |
| Tenka, no gems saved | 35% in her first month, 66% by the second |
| Tenka, 2 weeks of gems saved | 60% / 77% |

The current pace of patch-note gifts adds up to about 1.8× these pulls, so the real odds are better.
