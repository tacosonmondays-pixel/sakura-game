# Build Coach: "Set as Main" and her road to max (build spec)

Sakura Sentinels, 2026-10-30, revision 2 (review r2's L25: the awaken costs, the tab names, Main beside the Secretary,
the bench's sampling). Spec only. Companion to `docs/specs/GEAR_UPDATE.md` (GU) and `docs/specs/ACCESSORIES.md`; the
owner question is on `docs/review/gear-update/OWNER_BOARD.md` (Q18).

**Owner design (Oct 30, through the coordinator).** The player sets a **main girl**. A coach then shows everything she
still needs to max (level, stars and awakening, Potential, gear enhancement) as exact material counts; the best stages
and modes to farm each material, from the drop tables, with a Go button; which set, the main stat per slot, the
substat priority and the Tie and Collar Pin targets; which clubs to join or split; her Potential nodes and her LR
accessory; and a **build score** against her best, with "next best step" tips. The recommendations come from measured
bench data per girl and path, refreshed each balance round. It has its own milestone and needs no sim change.

**Words.** The button says **"Set as Main"**, never "pin": Tie and Collar Pins are gear items (GU §6), and profile
pins are trophies (`PINS_MAX`, `src/online/profileRules.js:15`). The feature is always the **Build Coach**, and every
file and save key it adds carries the `buildCoach` prefix: "coach" already means the tutorial's coach
(`src/ui/story/coach.js`, `src/ui/story/coachSteps.js`, `src/ui/styles/coach.css`, `tests/story/coach.test.js`) and a
girl-aimed ability ("Tap the girl … should coach", `src/ui/battle/aimControl.js:33`).

**Sources.** `file:line` is `origin/main` at `60ce9cad`. No other game is named here; players are "they".

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 0. On one page

1. **Set as Main** on any girl's page; one main girl at a time.
2. A **Coach** tab on her page (one word, like Info, Level, Awaken, Gear and Tree), in five parts: **To max** (exact
   counts), **Farm** (best sources, with Go), **Gear plan** (set, mains, substats, pins, clubs, LR kind), **Potential**
   (her nodes; for an LR girl, her personal accessory) and **Score** (build score and the next best steps).
3. **No new element on the home**, which the owner wanted calm (v0.1.54 "Quiet Home"): the main girl's next step is one
   filler page of the home's one news chip ("Kage · next: Enhance her Ribbon to +10 ›"). The Secretary stays the girl
   on the home; Main and Secretary are independent (§1).
4. **Data, not guesses**: every recommendation comes from `src/data/buildCoachData.js`, generated by a bench tool per
   girl and path and refreshed in each balance round's checklist.
5. **No sim change**, no co-op change. The coach reads the save and the data and only navigates.
6. It plans with what players earn by playing: it never points to the Gem Shop or a banner (Q18). An LR girl's personal
   accessory shows as information only.
7. **Milestone C1**, after GU M2 (it needs sets); it grows with M3 (LR) and accessories A2 as data rows.

## 1. Set as Main

- One main girl per save. The button sits on her page header; a second tap on another girl moves the title, and "Clear
  Main" removes it. The main girl's tile wears a small "Main" tag in lists.
- **Main and the Secretary (L25).** The Secretary (`profile.secretary`, `src/systems/save.js:159`, `:611`; picked in the
  home's background picker, `src/ui/lobby/bgPicker.js`) is who stands on the home and speaks; the Main girl is who the
  coach plans for. They are independent: "Set as Main" never changes the Secretary, a girl can be both, and the home
  shows the Main girl only as one page of its news chip (§8).
- Save: `profile.buildCoach = { main: unitId | null, seen: {} }` (additive). Mirror `cm:<unitId>` = 1 in
  `missions.counters` (no counter uses `cm:` today), so an older build that drops the block gives it back on the next
  load. `SAVE_VERSION` stays 2 (GU §9.4's `version: 3` marks exports only; this block needs nothing from it).

| Row | Counts from | Notes |

(The table `AWAKEN_COSTS`, `:413`, stays private, and `canAwaken`, `:433`, gives only the next star; L25) | to ★5 (UR); for an LR unit, the Limit Break fee and trial (`src/systems/limitBreak.js`) |
| Potential | `raiseCost` and `essenceBalance` (`src/systems/essence.js:75-88`) | P1–P6 |
| Gear | per slot: enhance coins to the piece's max (+10 / +12 / +18, `enhanceCost`, `src/systems/gear.js:519`), Rainbow Seeds, the Nightglass Lens, Needles for open ✦, the pins her plan names | follows her Gear plan (§4) |
| Accessory | an LR girl: her personal accessory's R step (information only); anyone: the standard accessory her plan names | ACCESSORIES.md |

A total line sums the coins. Rows she has finished collapse to a check mark.

## 3. Farm: where each missing material comes from

- For each missing item the coach lists its **best sources**, ranked by **expected units per run** (per energy once
  energy ships, `docs/specs/ENERGY_RESET.md`): stage drops (`src/data/stages.js` `drops`: chance × the count range, by
  difficulty), bounty arenas, Titan Trials tables (`src/systems/titanRewards.js`), the set sources (GU §3.6), exchange
  rows with their weekly caps (`src/data/shop.js`), the Workbench (`src/data/workbench.js`) and the Craft Room
  (`src/systems/craft.js`).
- It builds on `itemSources` (`src/systems/inventory.js:236`, which already gives "best sources first" and a stage id
  for Teleport) and adds the expected yield and whether the stage is open for this save (`isStageUnlocked`).
- Each source has **Go**: a stage opens its Stage Prep at that difficulty; an exchange opens its Mall tab; a Titan opens
  the Titan Trials hub on that titan.
- Gem Shop rows and banners are never listed (Q18).

## 4. Gear plan

From her row in `buildCoachData.js` (her best path first, a toggle for her other paths):

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Set** | her best set for her path, and the runner-up ("crowds: Party Popper") |
| **Main stat per slot** | five slots, the main she wants; her piece's main is checked or marked, with "Change with a Prism Dice" |
| **Substat priority** | an ordered list (for Kage: Crit DMG > Crit Rate > ATK > Speed), and per piece which lines match, which to reroll |
| **Pin targets** | which main gets the Tie Pin first, which line each Collar Pin |
| **Clubs** | the split to aim for ("Taiko 3 + Fencing 2"), and which pieces to re-tie with Club Armbands |
| **LR kind** | at the top: Set LR or Blank LR for her (GU §4.1), from the bench |
| **Accessory** | the standard accessory that fits her; an LR girl's personal accessory and what each R step does (information only) |

## 5. Potential

Her Potential Board nodes in the order the data recommends (`src/data/potentialBoards.js` `POTENTIAL_BOARDS`), the
Essence each needs (§2), and for an LR unit her LR step (`hasLrStep`).

## 6. Score and the next best step

- **Build score, 0–100**: her damage model with what she wears now ÷ the same model with **her best build of what she
  has** (the recommended set, clubs, mains and substats at her pieces' current rarities and levels, average rolls). A
  second line, **"Max"**, compares with her fully maxed bench build. Example: "Build 72 · Max 31".
- **The damage model** is per girl and path, fitted from bench runs: how her ruler (r5's measure: a boss for
  single-target girls, the late crowd for splash girls, `docs/BALANCE.md` on `feature/balance-r5`) responds to ATK,
  speed, crit, Pierce, Range and her set. Supports use their boost to allies.
- **Next best steps**: the three actions with the largest score gain per cost, each with Go: "Enhance her Ribbon +8 →
  +10 (+4, 18,000 coins)", "Prism Dice: Hair Clip main → ATK (+6)", "Tie an Armband: Taiko on her Shoes (+3: Taiko 3)".
  A step that needs something missing points to its Farm row.

## 7. Data and refresh

- `src/data/buildCoachData.js` (generated, not hashed): per girl and path: the set ranking, club split, mains per slot,
  substat weights, pin targets, LR kind, Potential order and the score model's coefficients; it records the sim id and
  balance round it was measured on.
- `src/systems/buildCoach.js` (new, not hashed: nothing under `src/sim` imports it): the pure logic, the "To max"
  counts, the farm ranking, the score and the next steps. It reads the save and the data and returns rows; the screens
  only draw them.
- `tools/balance/buildCoachBench.mjs` runs r5's bench harness (`tools/balance/r5Bench.mjs`, its `measure`) and writes
  the file. **Sampling (L25), not a grid.** For each girl and tier-5 path it starts from her bench build (the round's
  maxed profile in five pieces) and changes one thing at a time: +1 step of each stat her `GEAR_FIT` row calls real
  (about 8), each of the six sets at five pieces, and her top three club splits: about 18 builds plus the base, on 2
  targets (boss, crowd) × r5's 3 seeds, so about 115 runs a girl and path and about 11,000 for r5's 31 girls × 3 paths
  (an overnight run split by girl across cores). Mains per slot and the substat priority come from the fitted stat
  steps, and a candidate build is scored by the fitted model, not by a run of its own: a full grid (girl × path × set ×
  club split × mains) would be hundreds of thousands of runs. **Each balance round's checklist** (`docs/BALANCE.md`)
  gains "regenerate the coach data".
- A test fails when a playable girl has no row, and warns when the file's sim id differs from `simId()` (stale data).
