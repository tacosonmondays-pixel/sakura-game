# Achievements Expansion: build spec (v1)

Oct 15, 2026. Lands in the repo as `docs/specs/ACHIEVEMENTS.md`.

**Owner request (Oct 15, verbatim):** "Can you add more achievements some will grant you like gear boxes alongside gems and maybe even some ribbons? Special ribbons will have a cool effect to them!! And maybe titles too?"

**Inputs judged:**
- three research files: `research-systems.md` (RS), `research-economy.md` (RE), `research-metrics-cosmetics.md` (RM);
- three designs: `design-catalogue.md` (CAT), `design-economy.md` (ECON), `design-cosmetics.md` (COS);
- the approved Sensei Profile spec, `../profile/SPEC.md` (PS).

Where the designs disagree, §0.2 gives the ruling, and the rest of this spec follows it.

**Critic pass (Oct 15, later the same day).** Fixed against the real code: `a_journal_12` → `a_journal_11` (one of train A's 12 lessons is mail-only and can never be claimed), story chapters ignore scenes of girls who have not joined yet, `a_hero_25` moved to tier 2 (an Ornate box at tier 1 broke rule 4), launch-day counts (32 ribbons, 37 titles), Claim all repeats until nothing is left, and the mock moved to `mock/` with a Claim-all screen and an animated sheet of all 8 effects. The other numbers were recounted and stand.

## 1. Catalogue

### 1.1 Rules every row follows (pinned by tests, §7.3)

1. **Ids are permanent.**
   - Pattern `^a_[a-z0-9_]+$`, at most 24 characters. Every id is then a valid profile title id (`SQL:135`, `RULES:30`) and `ach:` pin (`SQL:193`, `RULES:34`).
   - Never rename, reuse or delete an id. Retire one with `retired: true`: hidden and not claimable, but it keeps its ribbon and title.
2. **Rewards are existing item ids only.**
   - Gems, coins, `gearbox_rare|superRare|mythic|legendary`, books, dice, Lock Pin, Star Fragment, tokens, `ticket_recruit`, `crown_dragon`.
   - Never `gearbox_common`, `ticket_recruit10` or `ticket_ssr_select`.
   - Never a ribbon or title as a reward entry. A cosmetic id in `rewards` would be dropped by older builds and flag the save "lossy" (`src/systems/save.js:313`, `:448`). It would also break `tests/systems/items.test.js:26`.
3. **Gems by tier.**

   | Tier | When | Gems |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | week 1 | 20–30 |
   | 2 | weeks 2–4 | 30–50 |
   | 3 | month 2 | 50–100 |
   | 4 | month 3+ | 100–200 |
   | 5 | capstone | 200–300 |
   | 0 | by rule | **none** |

   Tier 0 covers:
   - **grind steps**, which free unlimited taps can raise: sweeps add `clears` and `counters.sweep` (`src/systems/rewards.js:198-200`), and salvage and gear-received counts also grow from sweeps;
   - **steps whose event already pays gems once**: freeplay wave milestones (`src/online/coop/rewards.js:35-40`), the Matriarch milestone, story lessons and scenes;
   - the Codex mode experts, secrets, social, and the clan boss.
4. **Boxes by tier.**

   | Tier | Box |
   |---|---|
   | 0 | none, Ornate (×2) or Radiant, by the size of the step |
   | 1 | Sturdy, or no box (never better than Sturdy) |
   | 2 | Ornate |
   | 3 | Ornate ×2, or Radiant on headline steps |
   | 4 | Radiant |
   | 5 | Celestial; the one exception is Academy Anniversary (`a_login_365`), a calendar goal, which gives Radiant + Ornate ×2 |

   Celestial and Recruit Tickets appear only on tier 5. `final/check.mjs` enforces "Sturdy only at tier 1, nothing better at tier 1".
5. **Fixed goals.** No `UNITS.length` and no percentages in new rows (RS Risk 5).
6. **Nothing that signals spending or luck**, and nothing that pushes kids toward strangers:
   - no recruit counts, gems spent, Potential, duplicates or outfits;
   - no friend counts, messages or invites.

   Login steps count **days visited, never streaks**. `NO_COSMETIC(id)` (`a_pulls_*`, `a_units_all`, `a_reroll_10`) never gets a ribbon, title or pin.
7. **Names** are original, kid-safe, and about school life, the academy, sakura, the girls, or this game's enemies and places. The checker bans a word list that includes other games' names, gambling words, appearance words and mean words (`final/check.mjs`).

### 1.4 The catalogue

- **Metric** names are defined in §1.6 and counters in §1.7.

### 2.4 The live governor and its cost

`src/ui/honorFx.js` keeps one IntersectionObserver per screen root:

| Where | Specials live at once |
|---|---|
| Lists (Achievements tab, Ribbon Case) | **3** |
| Profile pin shelf | **4** |
| Detail view or fanfare | **1** large. Lists drop to **0** while a modal or overlay is open. |

- **Priority:** detail/fanfare > pins > ready rows > earned case cells.
- **The Case** moves a static "spotlight" to the next earned special every 6 s.
- **Losing `.live` means `animation: none`, never `paused`.** Paused animations kept their layers: 1,595 vs 76 (RM §3.2).
- **Live is cleared** when `document.hidden` is true and under both reduced-motion sources (`src/ui/components.js:164-171`).

**Measured on the mock** (`mock/tools/shoot.mjs`; the mock applies this governor's budgets by default):

| Profile (2 special pins) | 2 | 9 (includes the approved profile's UR shimmer) | 9 |
| Every screen under Reduce Motion | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **Overflow:** no horizontal overflow on any screen.
- **Cost (COS §2.5):** about 1–6 ms/s of main thread with 0 style recalcs per second at 4× CPU throttle.

**Rules:**
- No new top-level save key, no new item id, no `SAVE_VERSION` bump.
- The `fpm:` lesson is applied: anything that gates a payout lives in `missions.*`, never in a block an older build drops (`src/online/coop/rewards.js:221-237`; `TA:src/systems/patchGifts.js:5-9`).

### 3.2 Proof against older cached builds

**Probe** (`final/probe-save.mjs`). It runs the **live** `migrateProfile`, which is exactly what an older cached tab runs on a save this spec writes:

```
lossyBackup: false
claimsKept:  { a_first_win, a_quad_40, a_fp_bosses_4 }       // unknown ids kept
countersKept: { coopWin: 3, ach:flawless: 2, ach:seenHR: 4, ach:seenTT: 2, gear:legendary: 1, gear10:mythic: 2 ('2' → 2), eventWin: 7 }   // bad:true and obj dropped
newTopLevelBlockKept: false   // so ownership must never be a block (it isn't)
seenIntroKept: ['ach2']
olderBuildClaimOfNewId: 'unknown'
olderBuildListsNewId: false
```

| Situation | Result | Pays twice? |
|---|---|---|
| A new build claims; an older tab loads and saves | Claim, gems and boxes kept; the older tab can't claim the new id | **No** |
| An older tab loaded **before** the claim writes later | The whole profile is one JSON in one `setItem` (`src/core/store.js:89-98`), so claim and payout roll back **together**; the player can claim again **once** | **No** (existing rollback risk, not a new one) |
| Two new-build tabs both claim | Social's `saveRev` guard stops the stale tab (`SO:src/core/store.js:35`, `:102-115`); before social, last writer wins with one payment | **No** |
| M0 build (new rows hidden) after an M1 build claimed | Its claim markers are kept; M0 refuses claims of rows that aren't live yet (`'notLive'`) | **No** |
| A Codex row claimed on a CX build, then a pre-CX tab saves | `progress.stages[id].modes` is dropped (`src/systems/save.js:358-376`), so **unclaimed** ribbon progress can go back. The claim and its derived ribbon and title are safe. | **No** |
| Transfer code to a new device | The save forks, like every currency | Accepted (nothing server-side reads gems) |
| Import an older export, or RESET67 | The whole profile is replaced (`src/core/store.js:101-118`) | No net gain |

### 3.3 Claim gate (train A feature gates)

**The gate** in `claimAchievement` (ECON §4.6):
- any `gearbox_*` reward needs `featureOpen(p, 'gear')`;
- any `crown_*` reward needs `featureOpen(p, 'awaken')`.

Otherwise the claim returns `{ ok: false, error: 'locked', feature }`.

**How `featureOpen` behaves** (`TA:src/systems/story.js:445-452`):
- it returns true for legacy saves;
- it opens with the lesson (gear at 2-1, awaken at 3-2, `TA:src/data/story/lessons.js:172-175`) or with evidence of use (`TA:src/systems/story.js:429-438`).

**Effects:**
- `achievementList` returns `locked: 'gear' | 'awaken' | null`.
- Locked rows read "Claim after stage 2-1" (or 3-2). They light no badge and Claim all skips them.
- Veterans are never locked.

### 3.4 Permanence rules

1. **Ids** never change (§1.1).
2. **A shipped row's `rewards` never change.** Display text, `honor` and `title` may be added; only cosmetics are derived.
3. **Metrics may change only if they can only rise** for every save (R21). A goal may change only if it stays identical today (R22). Unclaimed progress must never go backwards; that is why `gearEver` and `gearMaxEver` take the max with a counter.
4. **Codex ribbon gems:** if CX ever turns them on, CX uses a numeric `mr:<stageId>` mask mirror in `missions.counters` (≈ 40 keys), **never** its planned `SAVE_VERSION` bump. A bump makes an older build start fresh and write over the save (`src/systems/save.js:472`; RS §1.6).
5. **A frozen ledger** (`tests/fixtures/achievements-ledger.json`) holds every shipped id with its rewards. Changing one takes a deliberate fixture edit.

### 3.5 Migration

**None.** A v0.1.27/v0.1.28 save loads unchanged:
- metrics and cosmetics are computed live;
- counters are created on demand by `ensure()` (`src/systems/missions.js:16-29`);
- `SAVE_VERSION` stays 2 (`src/systems/save.js:13`).

The only one-time step is the idempotent `seenIntro` `'ach2'` mark for the intro notice.

## 8. Owner questions (3, each with a default)

1. **Titles: hand-picked instead of "every achievement name".**
   - The approved profile spec turns every achievement name into a title. With 116 achievements that would include "Chapter 3 Complete".
   - This spec gives titles only where they read like a badge of honour: 45 in total, 37 at launch, on Standard, Gold and Prism plates.
   - **Default: yes, hand-picked.**
2. **Update day: pay everything veterans already earned, at once?**
   - A late-game player would claim about 2,790 gems and 56 gear boxes in one tap, and see 20 Honor Ribbons and 19 titles light up (12 ribbons and 9 titles come from achievements they claimed long ago). That is about 11 days of income; a mid-game player gets 990 gems.
   - **Default: yes, all at once.** No drip-feed and no "come back tomorrow".
3. **Show the Honor Ribbon count to visitors on the profile?**
   - It is one extra number on the public card, and it must be decided **before** the profile's server file is first pasted.
   - **Default: yes.**
