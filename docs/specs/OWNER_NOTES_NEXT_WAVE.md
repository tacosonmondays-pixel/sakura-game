# Rainbow gear: the plan in short

1. **Gold gear can awaken.** When a gold piece reaches +10, its "Max" button turns into a rainbow **Awaken** button.
   It costs **1 Rainbow Seed and 80,000 coins**, and it always works.
2. **Awakened gear is rainbow UR gear.**
   - It gets a turning rainbow border and a rainbow "UR" label, like your UR girls.
   - Every number on it grows by 14% (armor pierce by 10%), and it can level up to **+12**.
   - A short "Awakened to UR!" celebration plays when it awakens.
3. **The substat item is the Starlight Needle.** Pick any substat on a piece and **Stitch**: that substat grows by
   one step, shown as a ✦.
   - Gold gear at +10 takes one stitch.
   - Rainbow gear takes two: a gold ✦ and a rainbow ✦. Both can go on the same substat, for lines like
     **"+74% Crit DMG ✦✦"**.
4. **Nothing is ever lost.**
   - A stitch can be taken back for 10,000 coins, and the Needle comes back.
   - Rerolls never touch a stitched substat.
   - Breaking down rainbow gear gives the Seed and every Needle back.
   - Rainbow gear locks itself so it can't be broken down by accident.
5. **Stats get crazier, but stay fair.**
   - A girl in full rainbow gear does about **1.75× the damage of her best gold set**.
   - In today's Freeplay that is about 20 more waves on Crimson Moon, and none of our test teams reached the final
     Eclipse Wyrm.
   - Before it goes live we also test it with the stronger max upgrades being made now. If a team could then beat the
     Wyrm, we make the gear milder, never the Wyrm easier.
6. **Two new limits:** Crit DMG from gear stops at +200%, and Range stops at +50%. When a stat hits a limit, the game
   shows **MAX** and warns before a Needle goes on it.
7. **Pace.**
   - Seeds come from a new weekly task, "Rainbow Hunt" (clear the boss battles 3 times), plus a few one-time gifts.
     Needles come about 3 a week.
   - An everyday player gets about one rainbow piece a week: the first one in week 1 of the late game, a full set on
     one girl by week 3, and eight girls in about 5 months.
   - Seeds and Needles are never sold for gems, and they never come from random luck.
8. **New gear actions in the Backpack.** Spare pieces can be enhanced, rerolled, awakened and stitched there, without
   putting them on a girl first.
9. **It ships in two quiet steps.** First an update that teaches the game to understand rainbow gear (nothing changes
   on screen). Then, two weeks later, the update that turns it on. Co-op players don't need to update together for
   either step.
10. **Your public profile shows rainbow gear** with its ✦ marks. We need to adjust one rule on the profile server now,
    before it goes live.

## Other ideas, best first

  *(include now)*

- A "compared to what she wears" line on spare gear cards *(include now)*

- Gear presets per girl, plus a "Best for her" button *(next)*
- A Glimmer Needle that fixes one weak substat roll, plus a reroll luck meter that hands one out *(next)*
- Turn junk gold gear into rainbow dust for a weekly gear shop *(later)*
- Trade 3 gold pieces for 1 gold piece of your choice *(later)*
- School-club gear sets with small set bonuses *(later)*
- Name your rainbow pieces, and keep a gear album *(later)*
- A gear festival weekend event *(later)*
- A rainbow sparkle on the girl's 3D model *(last)*

# Gear: Pierce, no useless stats, and the Academy Club sets

The plan for your message: "more substat options … how about pierce? … different gear sets … I just dont want certain
ones to be useless". All power numbers are estimates from a model, not from real battles. They get measured in the big
battle update before anything ships.

**New substats**

1. **Pierce.** It rolls on ribbons. "+46% Pierce" means about half her attacks hit one more enemy. It works with
   every kind of attack:
   - Shots, beams and Shiro's sniper line go through one more enemy. Shiro, Momo and Miko get it at half strength,
     because one extra enemy doubles what a single-target shot hits and Shiro would otherwise need it on every map.
   - Blasts (Akane, Sango, Yuki, Midori, Nami) and Suzu's traps get bigger.
   - Sweeps (Rei, Hotaru, Hikari) hit 2 more enemies.
   - Raika's lightning jumps once more.
   - Chika's drones pierce.
   - Only Kaede's one-on-one duel can't use it, and for her it turns into ATK (point 4).
   - It's for crowds. Against a lone boss it does nothing, so players have a real choice between crowd gear and boss gear.
2. **Aura Power.** It rolls on shoes. It makes her aura stronger: a +10% damage aura becomes +11.5%. It's the first
   gear stat that helps Umeko and Miko do their actual job. Others who get an aura from a path or a Potential step
   (Hikari, Luna, Nami, Hotaru, Chika, Shiro) use it there.
3. Both new substats only appear on club pieces (point 8). Any piece without a club rolls and rerolls with exactly
   today's odds, old pieces included, so nobody's Fortune Dice get worse. Neither is ever a main stat, so an older copy
   of the game can never delete a whole piece because of them.

**No useless stats**

4. **Every Line Counts.** If a girl can't use a line, it still helps: it turns into ATK.
   - Examples: Ult Haste on a tower girl, crit on Suzu's traps, Range on Shiro (she already sees the whole map).
   - Each extra line like that adds a bit less than the one before, up to +12% ATK in total. One line gives about +5%,
     two give +7%. None ever adds zero.
   - Stats above their MAX (like 81% crit when the cap is 75%) turn into ATK too, heroes included, so nothing goes to
     waste.
   - The gear card prints the result, for example "Skill Haste 19% → +2.8% ATK".
   - It's decided from what the menu can see (her kit, stars and Potential). If an upgrade she buys in battle makes the
     line useful after all, she gets that on top. Nothing she buys in battle ever takes the ATK away.
   - Umeko and Miko are different: their own attacks barely matter, so their spare lines turn into a stronger and wider
     aura instead (up to +5%). That starts with the big battle update.
5. **Ult Haste becomes Skill Haste.** Every number stays the same. Heroes get exactly what they get today. When the
   tower ults (the ⚡ abilities) arrive, it also speeds up every girl's ability and the heroes' new skills, at half
   strength, and tower girls keep the ATK it already gives them. So nobody gets weaker on the day abilities ship. The
   few abilities that power up the whole team at once (like Luna's Moonbrand or Miko's Shrine Chorus) aren't sped up,
   because that would quietly raise every free player's boss damage.
6. Half of a girl's gear ATK also powers her burns and poisons (Akane, Midori), up to +25%, and Range also makes auras
   bigger.
7. Result: today 27 girl-and-stat pairs do nothing at all. After this change, none of the 190 pairs (19 girls × 10
   stats) do.

**Academy Club sets**

8. Purple (Mythic), gold and rainbow pieces can belong to a club. A Mythic set works exactly like a rainbow one, so
   free players get the full effect. With 2 pieces from the same club she gets a stat. With all 3 she also gets a
   **club trick**.
   - **Archery**: +30% Pierce. Trick: every 5th attack gets +100% Pierce.
   - **Fencing**: +3% Crit Rate. Trick: every 25th attack deals double damage and ignores armor.
   - **Taiko**: +5% Attack Speed. Trick: every 10th attack hits 50% harder (works on traps and drones too).
   - **Pyrotechnics**: +5% ATK. Trick: enemies she defeats explode and damage the ones nearby.
   - **Tea**: +6% Aura Power. Trick: girls in her aura attack 2% faster.
   - **Bell** comes with the tower abilities: +8% Skill Haste. Trick: her abilities start each wave 10% charged.
   - More clubs come later with new girls (Astronomy, Art and others).
   - Fencing and Taiko are the two boss clubs and come out about even: crit-heavy girls lean Fencing, fast attackers
     lean Taiko. Every girl has at least two clubs that suit her, except Umeko, who has one (Tea) for now.
9. **Getting clubs:**
   - Every new purple or better piece comes in a random club.
   - Gear from the weekly Titan Trials comes in the club the player picks.
   - The **Club Armband** moves any piece into the club you pick, old pieces included. It never changes a piece's stats.
   - Everyone gets 9 Armbands as a welcome gift, which is enough for three girls' full sets on day one. After that it's
     about 3–5 a week, all free. Armbands are never sold for gems.
   - Club pieces roll their club's three favourite substats twice as often, so a "perfect" piece can cost as little as a
     third of the Fortune Dice it does today. A piece in a club that doesn't fit what she wants is harder to perfect;
     that's what the Armband is for.
10. Prism Dice now let you **choose** the new main stat instead of rolling it at random.

**What a geared girl looks like**

11. Kage in gold +10 gear, all three pieces from the Archery Club:
    - **Pierce**: 76% (one 46% line plus 30% from the set). About 3 attacks in 4 go through one more enemy, and every
      5th attack gets another +100% on top.
    - **ATK**: her two Skill Haste lines and an Aura Power line turn into +8% ATK.
    - **Battle panel**: on best gold gear about 75k → 78k (81k in Taiko instead).
    - **When abilities arrive**: she keeps that ATK, and her two Skill Haste lines (38%) also cut Shadow Doubles' 40 s
      cooldown to about 32 s.
12. **How strong it is overall (estimates).**
    - A team's boss damage goes up about ×1.03 (month 3) to ×1.07 (month 6) from Every Line Counts alone.
    - With full club sets it goes up about ×1.15–1.2 for a free player at month 3, ×1.2 at month 6, and ×1.2 for a
      spender.
    - Every Freeplay target we set still holds in the model, and nobody gets anywhere near the wave-250 Eclipse Wyrm.
    - The one tight spot is two month-3 free players in co-op against the wave-200 Matriarch: the model puts them only
      1–4% short, so we treat it as a likely early win. If the real run shows they beat it, the tricks get a bit rarer
      first (every 30th / 12th attack), then the +12% ATK cap drops to +9%.

**When it ships**

13. **Step 1, with the first rainbow-gear update:**
    - the Skill Haste name;
    - Every Line Counts (as ATK);
    - choosing your Prism Dice stat;
    - "best on" portraits that show which of your girls a piece suits;
    - behind-the-scenes save safety.
    It needs no co-op update. One Freeplay test run checks it before it goes out.
14. **Step 2, with the big battle update** (the one with Potential v3, the path cap and the hero rework), at least two
    weeks later: Pierce, Aura Power, the five clubs and their tricks, Armbands, Umeko's and Miko's aura fill-in, and
    burns, poisons and auras growing with gear.
15. **Step 3, with the tower abilities:** Skill Haste speeds up the ⚡ abilities, plus the Bell Club.

**Questions** (we go with the default unless you say otherwise)

1. Should Skill Haste speed up every girl's ⚡ ability too, not only hero ults? *Default: yes, at half strength
   (cooldowns at most 25% shorter), not for the team-wide power-ups, and tower girls keep the ATK it gives them today.*
2. Lines a girl can't use turn into ATK, each one a bit less, up to +12% in total. Is that the right size? *Default:
   yes.* At +20%, free co-op pairs beat the wave-200 Matriarch too early. At +8%, a second useless line barely adds
   anything.
3. Two new substats now (Pierce, Aura Power), and maybe a third later? A "bonus damage to bosses" stat is the obvious
   next one, but it moves every boss wall at once. *Default: two now, and we decide on a third after the battle update
   is measured.*

# Essence, new level gates and the weekly Titan Trials

You asked: "When you get a duplicate character, you will recieve an essense, you will use that item to increase their
potential level! However leveling them will require a new set of items, including a ur crown from new bosses! Weekly
bosses are here … What else do you suggest". You also said yes to all ten of my ideas. This is the full plan with
every one of them in it.

## How it works

1. **A duplicate gives an Essence.** Every duplicate gives that girl **1 Essence**, whatever her rarity. Duplicates no
   longer give Star Fragments.
2. **You use Essence to raise her Potential.** Each step costs 1 Essence plus some coins. You press the button
   yourself, so it never happens on its own.
   - R girl: 2k, 4k, 6k, 10k, 15k coins (37k in all)
   - SR girl: 5k, 10k, 15k, 25k, 40k (95k in all)
   - SSR girl: 10k, 20k, 40k, 70k, 100k (240k in all)
   - The pace is the same as the Potential plan you approved. R girls reach P6 after about 30 recruits, a free SR in
     about a month, and SSRs stay rare.
3. **Each Potential step opens a new level gate.** Breaking it takes "a new set of items" from her own family
   (feathers, gears and so on) plus coins:

   | Gate | Level cap | Opens at | Needs |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 6 | 60 → 63 | P2 | 8 mythic, 2 legendary, 150k coins |
   | 7 | 63 → 67 | P3 | 10 mythic, 3 legendary, 1 Zenith, 200k |
   | 8 | 67 → 72 | P4 | 4 legendary, 2 Zenith, 300k |
   | 9 | 72 → 78 | P5 | 5 legendary, 3 Zenith, **1 Rainbow Crown**, 450k |
   | 10 | 78 → 85 | P6 | 6 legendary, 4 Zenith, **1 Rainbow Crown**, 800k |

   - **Zenith** is a brand-new top tier for every family: Zenith Plume (feathers), Zenith Cog (gears), Zenith Flame,
     Zenith Frost, Zenith Edge, Zenith Omamori and Zenith Sigil.
   - The **Rainbow Crown** is your UR Crown. 10 Rainbow Crown Shards fuse into one, with a little fanfare. It only
     comes from the titans, never from the gem shop, so it is the item everyone hunts.
   - Akashic Tomes still give the EXP in between gates: one Tome and 12k coins per level.
   - **It takes a lot of money:** about **2.2 million coins** to take one girl from 60 to 85. Gate 10 alone costs
     800k, the biggest single price in the game.
4. **Universal Essence, with rarities.** It is called **Kindred Essence** and comes as R, SR and SSR. The SSR one only
   comes as shards (10 shards make 1), so a free player gets about one extra SSR step by month 6. That keeps SSRs
   special.
5. **Extra Essence goes to the spark and a new shop.** Once a girl can't use any more Essence, each extra copy turns into:
   - **Spark points:** SR +1, SSR +5, and R +1 for every 5 copies.
   - **Mementos**, the simple currency, from R and SR copies.
   - **Moonpearls**, the special currency, from SR and SSR copies.

   Both are spent in the new **Memento Exchange**:
   - Mementos buy a standard Recruit Ticket, Star Fragments, materials and Tomes.
   - Moonpearls buy a premium 10× Recruit Ticket, a chosen SSR's Essence (the featured girl once banners arrive),
     Zenith items, Rainbow Crown Shards and a legendary gear box.
   - Stock is weekly, so it can never be farmed into endless free pulls. Free players get about 7% more SSRs a week
     from the extra spark points (at most 13% if they spend everything on tickets).
6. **Titan Trials, the weekly bosses.** Four giant bosses that today only appear past Freeplay wave 210:

   | Boss | Weak to | Drops (you pick one) |
   |---|---|---|
   | Foundry Titan | blast ×1.5 | gears or blades |
   | Gloom Regent | holy ×2 | runes or charms |
   | Hyakki Daimyo | holy | charms or ice |
   | Eclipse Wyrm | pierce ×1.5 | feathers or embers |

   - You tap the item family you need before the fight, and the game picks the one your next gate needs for you.
   - **12 keys a week** to spend on any boss. Up to 6 unused keys carry over to the next week. Titan Trials don't use
     energy.
   - **Tiers:** Normal → Hard → Nightmare → **Mayhem** → Mayhem +1, +2 … with no end, for the biggest teams. "Inferno"
     was already the name of Akane's top tier, so the last tier is called Mayhem.
   - Normal and Hard open early (after 3-5 and 6-5), so their rewards are sized for those chapters. The big drops start
     at Nightmare.
   - First clears pay gems (3,600 in all), crown shards, Zenith, gear boxes, and at Mayhem a whole Rainbow Crown.
7. **You get rewards even if she survives.** Hitting 25%, 50% or 75% of her health pays a Bronze, Silver or Gold
   chest. **Beating her adds the guaranteed Victory Chest:**
   - legendary materials of your family;
   - a chance at Zenith and Rainbow Crown Shards;
   - **one gear box.** On Mayhem it is a **UR (rainbow) box 4%** of the time (up to 6% at high Mayhem), a legendary box
     45%, and a mythic (your "SSR") box the rest. On Nightmare the UR box is a rare 0.5% surprise.
   - On Mayhem there is also a 1% chance of a **whole Rainbow Crown**.
   - **Bad-luck protection:** the Rainbow Meter on screen guarantees a UR gear box by the 40th Mayhem win. A UR box
     opens into real UR gear in the slot you choose.
8. **Every fight pays Assault Tokens, your boss currency.** The Boss Exchange gets a new **Titan shelf** that sells
   exactly what you're missing: a crown shard, Zenith, legendary of any family, Kindred Essence, an SSR shard every
   other week, Tomes and a legendary box. Spenders also get a small weekly shelf in the Gem Shop: Zenith and one SSR
   shard a week. Every row has a weekly limit.
9. **Craft Room:** turn 3 of a material into 1 of the next tier, or 2 of one family into 1 of another, for coins. (Not
   "Workshop", because the Workshop Yard already uses that name.)
10. **Co-op Titans** come in the next co-op update. You and a friend fight together and both get a **Together Chest**,
    and your clan gets a damage leaderboard on the Titan of the Week. Like co-op today, a co-op win doesn't pay
    first-clear rewards or unlock the next tier, so a strong friend can help you but can't carry you past the content.

## How long it takes

- **Free player:** four main girls reach level 75 around **month 3.5–4** and level 85 around **month 5.5**, and a team
  of eight is at level 85 around **month 7**. Coins and Rainbow Crowns are what take the longest. That matches the
  Potential plan: free players beat the 4-5 Matriarch around month 6, and wave 250 stays out of reach.
- **Spender:** nine girls reach level 85 in about **3.5 months** and all 19 in about **6.5 months**. After that they
  climb Mayhem +2 to +4 and chase the rare whole Rainbow Crown.
- **Once a free player's UR gear is done** (about month 7), they can reach the same boss tiers as a spender with the same
  team. Spenders stay ahead with SSR Potential and by getting there months sooner.
- **Star Fragments:** they now come from the bosses, the exchanges and small daily and weekly bonuses. Free players get
  about **95–145 a week**, compared with about 90–100 today, so awakening doesn't slow down. Big spenders get about
  **205 a week** instead of about 265, because duplicates no longer pay fragments. Their extra copies go into Essence
  and Potential instead.
- **Your duplicates are safe.** Every duplicate already counted for a girl becomes her Potential for free on day one,
  as the last patch note promised. Everyone also gets a welcome pouch of Mementos and Moonpearls.

## What else I suggest

1. **Auto-pick:** the boss screen picks the boss and family your next gate needs ("Kage needs 2 Zenith Plume → Eclipse
   Wyrm"). *In this update.*
2. **"Needed for" tags** on drops and shop items ("Needed: Kage Gate 9"), so kids always know what an item is for.
   *In this update.*
3. **One weekly boss habit:** the weekly rainbow gear mission also counts Titan Trials wins. *In this update.*
4. **Titan of the Week:** one boss pays +50% tokens each week and is the clan board's boss. *In this update.*
5. **Exciting recruit results:** "+1 Essence · P4 ready!" on every duplicate. *In this update.*
6. **Weekly boss moods:** a simple twist each week, like "Moonlit: holy +25%", so a different team shines. *Later.*
7. **Borrow a clanmate's girl** once a week for a titan fight. *Later.*
8. **Late-starter boost:** double tokens for the first 4 weeks after you unlock Titan Trials. *Later.*
9. **Potential memory scenes:** each Potential step unlocks a short "she trains harder" scene. *Later.*
10. **Prestige:** "Titan Vanquisher" titles, Mayhem frames, monthly clan seasons, and a little rainbow crown on girls
    past gate 10. *Later.*

## Questions (the build goes ahead on the default)

1. **Should the Eclipse Wyrm be a weekly boss?** It's the free team's favourite because it is weak to pierce, but it is
   also the wave-250 summit. *Default: yes. The weekly version is much smaller, and the summit stays out of reach.*
2. **Is the spender shelf in the Gem Shop OK?** One SSR shard a week for 1,500 gems and two Zenith a week for 250 each.
   It never sells crowns, crown shards, keys or UR boxes. *Default: yes, with the weekly limits.*
3. **Is the pace right?** Main girls at level 85 around month 5.5, a free team of eight around month 7, and gate 10 at
   800k coins. *Default: yes.*
