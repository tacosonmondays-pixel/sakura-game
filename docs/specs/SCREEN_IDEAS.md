# Reference screenshot study: ideas for Sakura Sentinels

Oct 20, 2026.

On Oct 20 the owner sent 13 screenshots of another tower defense game, covering its menus, store, events, modes, map picker, profile, settings and home screen. This study checks each idea in them against what Sakura Sentinels already has (live v0.1.36) and what is already planned.

**Summary**

- **104 ideas** in total. **16** are already in the game and **15** are already planned.
- **31** can be taken nearly as they are (in our own look). **64** are taken and changed so they fit academy girls. **9** are skipped.
- We can learn from ideas and systems. Names, logos, characters, icons, art, exact layouts and text from the reference game are not ours. Every idea below has our own name and look.
- No player name, ID, friend code, level, balance or other account detail from the screenshots is recorded here.

**How to read it**

- Job size: **XS** = a few hours, **S** = 1-2 days, **M** = 3-5 days, **L** = 1-2 weeks, **XL** = several weeks.
- The tags (A01, C04...) match the full idea table that came with this study.
- "Our plan" means the current tracks: **Tower abilities + new girls**, **UR gear**, **Ascension + weekly bosses**, **UI cleanup**, **Transitions + loading screen**, and then **Events + city hub**.

## 1. What's in the screenshots

**Image 1 (1a212b9c): co-op lobby, picking a difficulty.** Three big cards (Easy, Medium, Hard) sit over a blurred picture of the chosen map. A mascot fronts each card and gets scarier from left to right: a baby in a hood, a helmeted soldier, then a red-eyed robot. Each card has a bronze, silver or gold medal in the corner and shows the cash you win. The four co-op players run down the right edge, each with an avatar and a level badge that changes shape as the level grows. At the bottom left are a "change hero" portrait and a paid double-cash boost, switched on. *Worth noticing:* you can tell how hard each choice is before you read a word.

**Image 2 (3cd78ed0): the store's battle-items tab.** This tab sells single-use battle items: a farmer, a beacon, a cash pile, a stopwatch, a crate and more. They sit in a 3-column grid of coloured hexagons, with "New" ribbons and a small count of how many you own. On the right, the chosen item gets a big picture on a sunburst, its name, three lines on what it does and one big buy button. Across the top are tabs, with a red "!" on the tab you haven't opened. A second row of sub-tabs splits the single-use items from a set of permanent, reusable ones, and an "i" button explains the store. The cash balance has a big "+" next to it, and a banner sells a licensed crossover pack. *Worth noticing:* one item, one sheet, one button.

**Image 3 (45539a9f): weekly race event.** A weekly speed race on one fixed map and mode. One sheet shows when the race started and how long is left, the map and its difficulty and mode, and a crossed-out rule icon on the map (a feature that is switched off). A sideways strip shows the only towers you may use, some capped at 1, 2 or 3. Every badge and trophy you can win is shown before you play, a trophy counter sits in the header, and the tower strip ends on a half-cut card so you can tell it scrolls. Along the bottom are a leaderboard button, a big green start button, a "1 free entry" label and a paid pass for more tries. *Worth noticing:* the rules, the prizes and the clock all fit on one screen.

**Image 4 (4851f142): modes for the hard difficulty.** A two-line rules blurb explains the difficulty: towers cost more, you start later, enemies are faster, you get a set number of lives, and clearing the last round earns gold. Below it the modes are drawn as a path. Standard is the starting node. Sandbox branches off to its left, and to the right Standard splits into two rows of three harder modes each:

- Upper branch: one tower class only → double-HP big enemies → half cash.
- Lower branch: remixed rounds → a mode where you can't lose a single life → a "no help at all" prestige mode.

Every mode shows its own medal and its reward, and the hardest modes pay more. *Worth noticing:* the modes feel like a journey, not a list.

**Image 5 (6e26038f): map select.** Maps come in pages of six cards (3 by 2), with arrows, page dots, a search button and a friends button. Each card shows:

- a top-down picture of the track;
- the medals you've earned, grouped by difficulty, with empty outlines for the missing ones;
- a friend's face with their best score.

Locked maps get a padlock and an unlock prompt. A map with a saved game gets a green save badge, and maps with every medal get a darker frame than unfinished ones. Tabs along the bottom run beginner to expert plus a community tab, some with "New" ribbons. *Worth noticing:* each card tells you what you've done there and what a friend did.

**Image 6 (7b02faab): settings.** The settings sit over a blurred home scene. There is a jukebox for picking your music, with an on/off switch. There are three volume sliders: music, sound effects, and a separate one just for tower attacks. Each slider shows its percent, and a muted one gets a red slash. Below them is a row of big icon tiles: backup, account, logout, language, accessibility, notifications, extras and support. An "update notes" portrait badge sits at the bottom left. The footer shows the version and the player ID (not copied here). *Worth noticing:* the third slider just for attacks, and the fun extras tile.

**Image 7 (91547d15): tower roster.** Seven tower cards for one class. Each card has big art, a name and an XP total that grows every time you use that tower. The newest tower sits at 0. Four round class tabs at the bottom each use their class colour, and one has a "!" for something new. A big arrow on the left edge pages to the neighbouring section. *Worth noticing:* a simple "how much have I used her" number gives a reason to play everyone.

**Image 8 (94a0654d): cosmetic inventory.** A purple theatre stage with tabs: all, heroes, towers, enemies, co-op, game & UI, and favourites. The grid holds what you own:

- a profile avatar;
- a pet that sits by one tower type;
- a dozen packs of hats and props for the enemies: beards, bunny ears, cowboy hats, party hats, googly eyes and more.

Each tile shows a type badge, a green tick if it's switched on and a heart for favourites. The bottom sheet shows a big icon, a joke flavour text and enable / favourite buttons. An "i" button sits by the top tabs, the trophy balance sits top right, and a tab on the left edge pulls out a side drawer. *Worth noticing:* every pack has its own on/off switch (here one hat pack and the pet are on), so players mix their own look, and hats give a lot of variety for very little art.

**Image 9 (b68279a9): account-wide perk trees.** Six big category medallions stand on pedestals: four tower classes, heroes, and battle items. Each has an owned/total counter. The two currencies sit at the top right, each with a "+". At the bottom are an on/off switch for all the perks and a "respec all" button that costs in-game cash. *Worth noticing:* these are small permanent perks you earn by playing, and you can switch them off for fair runs.

**Image 10 (d5d52044): hero select.** On the left is a grid of hero portraits with a collection counter. Unseen heroes have "!" dots, and one locked hero shows a price. On the right is a big idle render of the chosen hero on a patterned backdrop, with a slanted name ribbon and a two-word title. Under it are "selected", the hero's cost in a match and a three-line bio: who they are, what they do, and whether they go on land or water. A "New" lore scroll and a button for levels and skins sit beside the bio. *Worth noticing:* the grid and the big preview share one screen, and there's a tiny lore unlock.

**Image 11 (db2b4a34): cosmetic store, featured tab.** Same stage and tabs as image 8. Fourteen cards are for sale, paid only with trophies you earn by playing, never with money:

- many avatars, all at 50;
- one pricier profile banner (300);
- a wind-up toy version of a tower (120);
- a pet, and a flag planted next to a tower (40);
- two hero placement effects: one for 70, and a fiery one that is already owned.

Cards show price chips, type icons and "New" ribbons. Owned cards turn blue and get a heart. *Worth noticing:* a currency you can only earn, spent only on looks, and priced by how visible each item is.

**Image 12 (ede06dda): player profile.** The profile has a notebook-doodle banner, an avatar and the account level at its maximum. Next to it, a second rank badge has its own XP bar that keeps going past max. There are also:

- an online pill, an eye button to see the profile as a visitor, a public-stats button and a followers box;
- a wall of medals with counts (bronze, silver, gold and skull families, plus rare medals and boss medals);
- top heroes and top towers with usage counts;
- a stats list with a category dropdown, a share tick for each stat and a count of how many are shared;
- a list of heroes and hero skins down the right side that filters the stats;
- small edit pencils on the banner and the picture, and a settings cog.

No name or number from it is recorded here. *Worth noticing:* the second rank bar, and choosing which stats other people see.

**Image 13 (fa0345d6): home screen.** The home screen is a little island where each building or prop opens a feature:

- the clock-tower hut: daily challenge;
- the chest: free chest;
- the explorer with a "!": quests;
- the gift box: gifts;
- two characters on tablets: social;
- the race flag, the team banner and the brazier: events;
- an old TV: watch an ad.

Round event buttons run down the right edge. The store and the cosmetic store sit on the left, and mail with a "!" sits at the top. A bottom bar of round tiles holds towers, heroes, a roguelike mode, a big green play button, social play, items, and the perk trees. Many tiles have "New" ribbons, and one shows the newest hero's face. *Worth noticing:* the home screen is a place, and it shows you what's new.

## 2. Take it (in our style)

These ideas are plain good sense for a game UI, so we can take them nearly as they are. Only the look and the names change: white academy panels, sakura pink and gold, slim tech lines, our own line icons. Ideas from this group that we already have are listed in section 5.

**"What's new" badges**

- **Unread dots that roll up, plus NEW tags** `E04`. Today each "!" dot works on its own. Instead, one shared list of what you've already seen drives every dot. A small sakura-pink dot (with a number when it counts things, not their red "!" circle) climbs from the card to its tab to the lobby tile, and new things get a small NEW pill in our tech font in the top-right corner, not a slanted ribbon. The Captain's Journal, the Story archive and recruit results already show a NEW tag, so this reuses that look. A dot clears once you've seen the thing, and a long-press on a tab gives "Mark all seen". A dot only shows when there is really something to see or claim. *M.* This is the most useful idea in the study, and the 22 new girls need it.
- **Owned / total counters** `E05`. "Enrolled 19/41" on Students, "Guardians 3/3", "x/y" on each cosmetics tab, and ring meters on the Curriculum boards. *XS.*
- **A "What's New" nudge from the secretary** `G09`. For 3 days after an update, the Notice button gets a NEW pill, the secretary's lobby speech bubble mentions the update, and Settings > About gets a "What's new" row. Each one opens the Notice's patch notes. We skip their round portrait badge in the corner. *XS.*

**Before a battle**

- **An exact rules line for each difficulty** `A03`. One line is built from the game's own difficulty numbers, so it can never go out of date. Example: "Hard: girls cost +8%, enemies +35% HP and +10% speed, 50 hearts, coins x0.95. Win on Standard for the gold medal." *XS.*
- **Practice this stage in Sandbox** `A13`. A small "Practice in Sandbox" button beside the Standard stop on the mode map opens Sandbox on this same stage. *XS.*
- **A medal shelf on every stage card** `A14`. The 4 medals become small pips, with tiny mode-ribbon dots under each one. Unearned ones show as outlines, and a tap opens the full breakdown. *S.*
- **A hero chip on every pre-battle screen** `A24`. The hero slot already in stage prep's formation panel becomes a chip with the hero's portrait and "FREE · Lv 1-25", and the same chip goes on the mode map and the co-op player cards. It sits inside our own panels, not as a portrait badge in the bottom-left corner. A tap opens a quick sheet of our heroes and their roles (Vanguard / Mystic / Tideguard) without leaving the screen. The chosen hero gets a mint "On duty" pill. *S.*
- **Map search with filters** `A21`. Search by stage name or theme, and filter by missing medals, map rule, freeplay best or district. *S.* It becomes more useful once the number of stages doubles.

**In battle**

- **Call the next wave early for bonus coins** `C02`. A "Next wave" button appears once the current wave has fully come out. You can only be one wave ahead, and each early call pays bonus coins. In co-op both players agree (or the host decides). *M.* Any speed race needs this first.

**Store and items**

- **A "+" on every currency** `B06`. For coins and tokens, the "+" opens a "How to earn" sheet with Go buttons (Coin Bounty, Commissions, login gift). For gems it still opens the Gem Shop. *XS.*
- **A clear item card** `B08`. Each card is a rounded tile with:
  - a rarity glow and an "x3" count chip;
  - a mint "worn" tick;
  - a line-icon type glyph;
  - a sakura pin star for favourites and a pink NEW dot.

  At x0 the card greys out and shows its price. It uses our rarity colours, not their hexagons. *S.*
- **An "i" button that explains each system** `B10`. It goes on the Mall tabs, the Merit Exchange, gadgets and the Curriculum. Each opens a short sheet: where to get things, Go buttons and a wiki link. *XS.*

**Roster**

- **Swipe, and chevrons for role tabs** `E07`. Her page already has ‹ › buttons that cycle girls. We add swipe on her page, and the same thin chevrons plus swipe to move between role tabs on Students. *XS.*

**Settings and style**

- **Separate volume for girls' attacks and for alerts** `G02`. Two new sliders:
  - "Girls' attacks";
  - "Alerts & UI": wave start, leaks, the boss warning and "ability ready".

  "Voices" comes later. A muted icon dims behind a diagonal pink slash. Late waves stay readable by ear. *S.*
- **Language picker** `G05`. For later: each language is named in its own script, with no flags. The first step is moving all game text into one place. *L.*
- **The back arrow is always in the same spot** `G11`. We fix the pop-ups that still close somewhere else. Esc and the phone's back button do the same thing. *XS.*
- **Two rules for the style guide** `G12`. First, each screen gets one main button (our yellow primary, which the UI cleanup keeps), an outlined second button and flat status chips. Second, a sideways strip ends on a half-cut card so people know it scrolls. *XS.* These go into the UI cleanup style guide.

## 3. Take it and change it

For each idea: what we saw, then our version with its name, look and rules. All of it is themed for the academy: clubs, school events, the Student Council, the Sensei.

### Before a battle

- **Danger Level cards** `A01`. *Seen:* difficulty cards fronted by a mascot that gets scarier. *Ours:* our own monsters front each difficulty. Easy is a sleepy jelly slime, Normal a goblin scout, Hard an armored oni, and Nightmare a shadow-dragon hatchling with a purple glow. Each is a white academy panel with a slanted tech strip in the difficulty colour. The monster stands full-body inside the panel, rendered from our own enemy models, instead of a round portrait sitting on top of the name, and the bronze, silver, gold or sakura medal sits in the strip next to the name instead of on the card's corner. All four fit one row on a phone. *S.*
- **Frosted glass menus** `A04`. *Seen:* menus over a blurred picture of the map. *Ours:* frosted glass over the stage's own blurred thumbnail, with a faint diagonal scan-line tint (our urban-tech touch). On Low quality we use a pre-blurred picture instead, so phones stay fast. *S.*
- **The Elective Path** `A05`. *Seen:* modes drawn as a branching path. *Ours:*
  - The path: a campus transit map. Standard is the central station. The teal line runs Reverse Route → Goblin Rush → Ironclad, the pink line runs Half Income → Last Petal, and both lines meet at Graduation Exam, the last stop. That is our own shape, not two rows of three with arrows between them.
  - The look: glass station dots on thin neon lines, with no arrows between stops. Each stop wears its ribbon glyph (looping arrow, speed lines, riveted shield, half coin purse, single petal), outlined until earned. Sandbox is a small side platform off Standard.
  - The rule: your rule stays, and every mode unlocks after one clear. The path only sets the order and the look. It adds no new locks unless you want a chain.

  *M.*
- **Harder modes pay a bit extra** `A06`. *Seen:* harder modes pay more. *Ours:* we keep your Oct 15 rule that modes pay ribbons, not gems. On top, Merit Stamps (our new cosmetic currency, see Events) pay out at ribbon milestones on a stage (5 / 10 / 20 ribbons) and for a Graduation Exam clear. *XS.*
- **Roster Rules and Club Day** `A07`. *Seen:* a race where only listed towers may be used, some capped, and a "one class only" mode. *Ours:*
  - A Roster Rules strip shows the allowed girls as portrait cards edged in our role colours: DPS red, Control cyan, Support green, Frag orange, Economy gold, Hero purple. Some carry "x2" cap chips.
  - Every allowed girl can be borrowed as a Loaner at a fixed baseline, so owning her never decides a ranking.
  - Club Day means only one role may be placed (DPS Day, Control Day...).

  *M.*
- **Heavyweights** `A08`. *Seen:* a double-HP big-enemies mode. *Ours:* bosses, minibosses and elites get double HP. It ships as a map rule or a weekly twist (like the Titan "moods" in the Ascension plan), not as a seventh mode. *XS.*
- **Pop Quiz** `A10`. *Seen:* a remixed-rounds mode. *Ours:* the same stage with a remixed wave list from our bestiary. That can mean other monster families, early elites, surprise flyers and veiled waves. The remix uses only families that chapter has already shown, and it's the same for everyone, so co-op works. *M.*
- **Graduation Exam** `A12`. *Seen:* a "no help at all" prestige mode with its own medal. *Ours:* the rules are:
  - no Pocket Gadgets and no Sensei Commands;
  - no selling and no income from Economy girls;
  - 1 heart;
  - girls at a fixed baseline: no gear, Potential or awakening.

  It pays a black-and-gold exam ribbon and a step toward the stage's Gold Leaf frame. *M.*
- **Gold Leaf frame** `A15`. *Seen:* fully finished maps get a different card frame. *Ours:* the stage card border turns sakura-gold with a slow shimmer once every medal and ribbon on it is earned. It counts toward an achievement. *XS.*
- **Map browser** `A16`. *Seen:* pages of map cards with difficulty tabs and New flags. *Ours:* the co-op, Sandbox and freeplay pickers keep a scrolling grid of our stage cards (the campaign keeps its chapters), with tier filter chips in the top bar that use our own icons (sprout → blossom → storm cloud → oni mask) and NEW tags. We skip their 3x2 pages with page dots and big round tier tabs along the bottom. Once the city exists, each map gets a city-district chip. *S.*
- **Classmate Best** `A22`. *Seen:* a friend's face and best score on each map. *Ours:* each stage card shows the friend or clanmate with the best freeplay wave there, with the wave number. A tap opens a small friends + clan ranking. It shows on locked stages too, as a nudge to unlock them. Only friends and clan appear, and each player's profile privacy is respected. *M.*
- **Active Bonuses chip** `A25`. *Seen:* a paid double-cash boost. *Ours:* one chip in stage prep's header lists what applies to this battle: weekly double drops, event bonus girls and the energy cost. We never sell extra battle cash, which keeps co-op and rankings fair. *XS.*
- **Room plates in co-op** `A26`. *Seen:* a column of the room's players with level badges down the screen edge. *Ours:* the co-op room's existing player cards become Room plates. Each plate shows an avatar and frame, a Sensei Crest, a title chip, DEV / ALPHA tags, a ready tick and the hero chip. There are 2 plates now and a row of 4 once 4-player co-op lands, not a column of round avatars down the edge. *S.*

### Store and items

- **Pocket Gadgets** `B01`. *Seen:* single-use battle items, sold in packs. *Ours:* the Engineering Club's kit, earned only and never sold.
  - The six gadgets:
    - Caution Tape holds the next few enemies on the road.
    - Freeze Spray slows the whole map for 2-3 seconds.
    - Cheer Megaphone gives +20% attack speed nearby for 10 seconds.
    - Lunch Money gives extra coins. It's off where there's no income and halved in Half Income.
    - First-Aid Kit gives +5 hearts. It's off where healing is banned.
    - Flashlight reveals veiled enemies in an area.
  - Limits: up to 3 per battle, and 1 of each per wave.
  - Where they come from: event shops, objectives and the Bounty Exchange.
  - Where they're banned: Graduation Exam, ranked events and Nightmare medal runs. A run that used one shows "assisted".

  *L.*
- **Sensei Commands** `B02`. *Seen:* permanent powers you can reuse. *Ours:* you, the Sensei, equip one command and use it once per battle:
  - Morning Bell: every enemy staggers for 1.5 seconds.
  - Pep Rally: all girls get +15% attack speed for 10 seconds.
  - Transfer Slip: refunds the last girl you sold in full.

  Commands unlock through Sensei level and achievements and are never bought. *L.* They come after tower abilities.
- **Event gadget** `B04`. *Seen:* a limited crossover item that powers up the crossover heroes. *Ours:* for the summer event, a "Tide Lantern" sends a wave that knocks enemies back. While it glows, Marina and Amane get +20% attack speed. It's bought with event currency in the event shop and disappears when the event ends. No licensed characters. *M.*
- **Club Bundle card** `B05`. *Seen:* a featured bundle banner. *Ours:* a bundle of event gadgets, a cosmetic and a recruit ticket, for gems or event currency, with a countdown. It never holds power you can't get elsewhere. *S.*
- **Mall tab groups** `B07`. *Seen:* top tabs with sub-tabs. *Ours:* the top tabs become Featured / Gadgets / Recruit / Exchanges (Boss, Bounty, Memento) / Wardrobe. Every exchange we already have stays inside. *S.*
- **Detail sheet** `B09`. *Seen:* an item panel with one big button and a joke flavour text. *Ours:* a sheet on the right (a bottom sheet on phones) with:
  - the art on a clean white card with a thin rarity-colour frame, with no sunburst behind it;
  - what the item does, with real numbers;
  - "Allowed in" mode chips, "Owned xN", and Buy x1 / x5;
  - one or two lines of wholesome academy flavour, e.g. "The Art Club knitted this hat for the slime that keeps visiting the courtyard."

  *S.*

### Events (re-skinned as school events)

- **Sports Day Cup** `C01`. *Seen:* a weekly race on a fixed map, mode and tower list, with a leaderboard. *Ours:* the academy's sports-festival week.
  - Each week has one stage, one mode and one set of Roster Rules, starting at the 7 PM Central reset.
  - Your score is how fast you clear. The server re-plays your moves to check it, so a score can't be faked.
  - You get 3 free ranked runs a day, plus an unlimited unranked Practice Lane that shows where your time would place. Extra ranked runs come from a ticket you earn, never from gems.
  - The header is a tablet-style "event dossier" with corner brackets: a countdown, the stage, a "Normal · Rush" chip, your best time, and one of our girls in a track-club jacket holding a stopwatch.

  *L.*
- **Cup Crests** `C03`. *Seen:* every reward badge shown before you play. *Ours:* our own ladder, not theirs: a gold sakura crest for the winner, a silver one for 2nd-3rd, then Top 10, Top 100, Top 5%, Top 20% and Top 50%, plus a small "Ran the course" stamp for any finish. Each crest becomes a line on your profile and pays Merit Stamps. *S.*
- **Merit Stamps and the Merit Exchange** `C04`. *Seen:* a cosmetic store paid with trophies you earn by playing. *Ours:* a pink-and-gold school merit stamp.
  - You earn stamps from achievements, medal and ribbon milestones, Titan Trials crests, event ranks, Cup Crests and clan weekly goals.
  - Stamps buy only cosmetics: avatars, banners, frames, mascots, monster props, effects and radio tracks. They are never sold, never turned into gems and never buy power.
  - A Featured shelf rotates each week at 7 PM, with NEW tags and a countdown. A shortcut sits in the Mall's tabs and in the lobby menu.

  *M*, plus art.
- **Event cards** `C05`. *Seen:* round event buttons down the home screen's edge. *Ours:* the lobby's existing carousel and Events banner become a stack of up to 3 slim slanted event cards: the season event, Titan of the Week and the Sports Day Cup. Each shows a countdown and a state chip ("Free try left", "Done"). They are rectangular academy cards in our layout, not round medallions down the screen edge. *S.*
- **Clan Turf Week** `C06`. *Seen:* a team territory event. *Ours:* clans claim tiles of a city map. Each tile is a mini-stage with a score to beat, and clan banners fly on the tiles a clan holds. The weekly ranking pays clan XP and Merit Stamps. *XL*, after the city.
- **Field Trip** `C07`. *Seen:* an expedition across several maps. *Ours:* 3-7 stages in a row on a school bus with a set number of seats, so you pick your crew before leaving. Hearts carry over between stages, and a reward chest waits at the end. There's an Easy and a Hard trip each week. *L.*
- **Night Watch** `C08`. *Seen:* a roguelike mode. *Ours:* a node map of the city at night. You start with 3 girls, draft more after fights and pick run perks called Glowsticks (each one a coloured light with a small run bonus). A boss waits at the end, and wins unlock cosmetics. *XL*, after the city.
- **Daily Drill** `C09`. *Seen:* a daily challenge in a clock-tower hut. *Ours:* a clock-tower tile with one stage a day, picked at the 7 PM reset and played with a Loaner team. It pays small gems and Merit Stamps. It counts the days you visited and never punishes a broken streak. *M.*
- **Council Casebook** `C10`. *Seen:* a quest-giver with short story maps. *Ours:* the Student Council posts cases. Each is a short speech bubble from a girl plus a handmade twist, like "only water girls" or "protect the bakery stall". Cases reward a Diary page, an avatar and gems. *L.*

### Cosmetics

- **The Dressing Room** `D01`. *Seen:* store and inventory tabs, categories, favourites, a filter drawer, and several items switched on at once. *Ours:*
  - "Exchange / Owned" at the top, with tabs All, Girls, Heroes, Monsters, Co-op, Battle & Menu, Profile and Pinned (a sakura pin star, not hearts).
  - A filter drawer on the left: owned, source, girl, rarity, new.
  - "Wear" switches. If several monster hats are on, they show up at random. Items with one slot just swap.
  - "Shuffle my pinned lobby backgrounds daily".

  The planned Wardrobe tab for outfits links into it. *M.*
- **A boutique fitting room** `D02`. *Seen:* the cosmetic shop set on a theatre stage with purple curtains. *Ours:* no theatre at all, so we don't echo their stage. The Dressing Room is a fitting room in the academy Mall's boutique: a tall mirror with a soft ring light, a clothing rail, and a round holo-platform where your chosen girl stands in 3D wearing the preview. Glass panels sit over the painted room. *S.*
- **Club Mascots** `D03`. *Seen:* a pet that sits next to one tower. *Ours:* a tiny cosmetic buddy beside a girl, like an owl chick on Aoi's quiver, Hikari's star sprite, a shiba plush, or a robot cat for the tech girls. It bobs while idle, cheers when she reaches a tier or uses her ability, and naps between waves. It must never look like it fights. *L.*
- **Monster Dress-Up** `D04`. *Seen:* packs of hats and props for the enemies. *Ours:* props on our 12 monster families: festival fox masks, straw sun hats, sakura sprigs, scarves, autumn witch hats, winter earmuffs, and graduation caps for the academy anniversary. We leave out party hats and flower crowns, because those are packs in the screenshot. A prop never covers an armour or ward sign and never goes on bosses. Each co-op player sees their own choice. *M.*
- **Battle & Menu effects** `D05`. *Seen:* game and UI cosmetics, like a fiery placement effect. *Ours:*
  - placement effects: petal burst, chalk magic circle, holo grid ring;
  - defeat effects: sparkle pop, confetti, origami fold;
  - coin pickup sound packs and battle frame skins.

  They never change size or readability, and Reduce motion switches them off. *M.*

## 7. What we must not copy

We can learn from ideas and systems ("a store that only takes an earned currency", "modes drawn as a path"). We can't take anything below. Everything we build gets our own name, our own art and our own layout.

- **Names.** Nothing named in the reference screenshots goes into the game: not the game, its studio, or any of its tower, hero, enemy, mode, map, item, currency, store, pass or menu names. Every name in this study is ours and was checked against our own game.
- **Licensed crossovers.** The crossover pack in the screenshots and its characters belong to their owners. Our event items use our own girls and our own story.
- **Characters and likenesses.** None of the reference game's mascots, heroes or enemies, even redrawn as girls. No girl of ours should be a recognisable redraw of one of their heroes (same costume, colours, props and pose).
- **Art and icons.** Their painted scenes are out: the island home, the beach maps, the carved mascot heads, the wooden planks and the purple theatre curtains (and the theatre-stage setting itself) and the blue-ink notebook-doodle profile banner. So are their hexagon item tiles, star-on-ribbon and skull medals, the trophy cup, the banknote, the perk book and the double-cash coin. We draw our own: line icons, sakura and tech shapes, our own medals and our own monsters.
- **Exact layouts.** Taking the idea is fine. Copying the screen is not. Our difficulty cards, mode path, map pages, store grid, profile and home screen get our own arrangement, proportions and frames: white academy panels, glass, slim tech lines, slanted strips. We changed the closest ones on purpose: the mode map is a transit map, not two rows of three with arrows; map pickers scroll with filter chips in the top bar, not 3x2 pages with round tabs along the bottom; events are slim cards, not round medallions down the edge; the hero chip lives in the formation panel, not a corner portrait; settings use a section rail, not sliders over a row of tiles; Tenure ranks are stars on the crest rim, not a numbered star badge; and the Cup Crest ladder and the first monster hats are our own, not theirs.
- **Text.** No rules blurbs, item descriptions, joke flavour texts, hero bios or button labels from the screenshots. We write our own, in our academy voice.
- **Fonts, colours and sounds.** No bubbly outlined lettering, bright blue back buttons or green-on-wood sliders, and none of their sounds or music.
- **Their paid tricks.** No paid battle boosts, paid tries, paid perk points or ads (see section 4).
- **Player details.** No name, ID, friend code, level, balance or stat from any screenshot goes into the game, the repo or any page.
