# Achievements expansion: owner note

1. **102 new achievements.** 86 arrive with this update, so the game goes from 30 to 116. The other 16 arrive with the clan update, the Codex modes and later features (132 in all). Every part of the game gets an easy first goal in week one and a big goal months away. Ladders such as "Gold medals 1 / 10 / 20 / 40" show as one tidy row.

2. **Gear boxes alongside gems.** 85 of the new achievements give a gear box, and 67 of those give gems too. Harder goals give better boxes. The best box, Celestial, is only on the 5 hardest goals. No box can be claimed before stage 2-1, where equipment opens.

3. **Gems stay fair.** The new achievements pay about 5,900 gems at launch (about 6,800 once everything ships). That is less than one month of normal daily play, so recruiting isn't thrown off. The big prizes are the boxes, ribbons and titles.

4. **34 Honor Ribbons, each with its own name and art** (32 at launch). Plain, shiny and special ribbons have different shapes. There is one for every chapter, so every player earns one in the first hour, and players who already cleared chapters get theirs right away.

5. **8 special ribbons, each with its own cool effect:** a rainbow swirl, falling sakura petals, sunrise rays, a little lightning crackle, glowing embers, a drifting starfield, aurora lights and holographic foil (the foil one comes with the Codex modes). They are light on phones, and they hold still for anyone who turns on Reduce Motion. You can watch all 8 in `mock/shots/specials-anim.webp`.

6. **45 titles, 37 at launch,** shown under your name on the profile. There are three plate styles: standard, gold, and a rare rainbow-edged one. All are wholesome: nothing about looks, spending or luck, and nothing mean.

7. **Kid-friendly and safe.**
   - No new goal rewards recruiting, spending, adding friends or messaging.
   - Login goals count days visited, never streaks.
   - There are 6 secret achievements with gentle hints.
   - A reward can never be paid twice, even if an old browser tab is still open.

**Questions (the default is used if you don't answer):**

8. **Hand-picked titles instead of every achievement name?** The approved profile plan turns every achievement name into a title, which would include ones like "Chapter 3 Complete". *Default: yes, use the 45 hand-picked titles.*

9. **Give veterans everything they already earned on day one?** A late-game player would claim about 2,800 gems and 56 gear boxes in one tap, and see 20 ribbons and 19 titles light up. A mid-game player would get about 1,000 gems. *Default: yes, all at once, with no "come back tomorrow".*

10. **Show your Honor Ribbon count to visitors on your profile?** This has to be decided before the profile goes online. *Default: yes.*
