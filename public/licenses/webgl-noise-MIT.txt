webgl-noise (https://github.com/stegu/webgl-noise)

Used in: src/render/vfx/toonCore.js, toonCellular(): a JavaScript port of
src/cellular2D.glsl ("Cellular noise ("Worley noise") in 2D in GLSL. Copyright (c)
Stefan Gustavson 2011-04-19. ... released under the conditions of the MIT license"),
with the cell lattice wrapped to a period so the painted Worley F2 - F1 channel
tiles. The 3 x 3 search window, the permutation polynomial and the jitter are the
original's.
