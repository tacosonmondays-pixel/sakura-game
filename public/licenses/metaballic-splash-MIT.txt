"Procedural Metaballic Splash" by Alkaliii (godotshaders.com/shader/procedural-metaballic-splash/,
posted 2025-10-04; the post's licence tag: MIT)

Used in: src/render/vfx/water.js, the drops layer. Only the idea is used: feathered
fields summed and cut by one hard threshold ("clip"), so drops that are close are one
blob and part as they separate. The code is our own (no shader text copied); the
original's hash ("Hash without Sine") is not used.
