stylized-components by Christian Ortiz (github.com/cortiz2894/stylized-components)

Used in: src/render/vfx/water.js, the ripples (ground layer): rings spaced in time
that run out from the impact at a speed, with an exponential decay
(src/components/waterFloor/shaders/fragment.ts, "Ripple rings - hard-edged anime
rings per impact event"). Rewritten for our toon core: the decay drives a rising
erosion threshold (the ring breaks into dashes) instead of a fade, and the ring is a
light line inked on its outer edge.
