GDQuest godot-visual-effects (https://github.com/gdquest-demos/godot-visual-effects), its laser beam demo

Used in: src/render/vfx/toonBeams.js, the charge-up and the impact. Only ideas from the demo's code (MIT) are used,
re-implemented in our own GLSL on the toon core (no code taken): the beam appears by growing out from its muzzle and
leaves by thinning, with a gathering "casting" effect at the muzzle while it starts and a separate effect where it
collides (here: a ring gathering in and streaks converging on it at the muzzle over her wind-up, and at the hit point
an inked crown of tongues, a stepped ring and a glow pool on the ground). The repository's art (textures and models)
is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and none of it is used: no texture or model from it ships with the game.
