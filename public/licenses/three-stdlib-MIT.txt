three-stdlib (https://github.com/pmndrs/three-stdlib)

Used in: src/render/vfx/electric.js (the electricity crackle, its arcs and drum-beat rings): ideas from its
LightningStrike geometry, re-implemented in our own code (no code taken): a path built by halving its segments and
pushing each new joint sideways by a share of the segment that shrinks each halving; forks (subrays) leaving from
inner joints, turned off the parent's direction, shorter and thinner, with a limited recursion; each subray shown
for only part of its period (a duty cycle); a ray that grows from its source, then vanishes. The stepped re-strike,
the zig-zag of the last halving, the capsule the crackle hugs and the toon layers are ours.

three-stdlib's LightningStrike is itself a port of the LightningStrike geometry in the three.js examples
(https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, MIT), so the same ideas also come from there; that licence already ships with
the game's three dependency.
